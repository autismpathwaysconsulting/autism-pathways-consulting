Website and service terms for Autism Pathways Consulting.

What APC Is and Is Not

Autism Pathways Consulting provides parent education, pattern review, practical support planning, and implementation guidance. Our services are not therapy, psychology, occupational therapy, speech therapy, or any form of medical or clinical treatment. We do not diagnose conditions, and we do not guarantee any specific outcome for your child. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists or professionals your child is already seeing.

Services

Availability notice: Paid support is subject to individual Founder suitability, capacity, and availability review. International clients may be accepted for online support under the same review process.

  • Free 15-Min First Step Call: a fit-and-routing conversation to check suitability, clarify service fit, identify when another professional is needed, and explain the next step. It is not a consultation, assessment, diagnostic service, advice session, or therapy.
  • One-Concern Parent Session (RM350): a 45-minute online session for one repeated concern. It includes a short pre-session concern form reviewed by CJ, focused pattern review, one cautious working hypothesis, one agreed priority, 1–2 practical changes to test, guidance on what to observe next, and one clarification message within 7 calendar days about the agreed concern or plan only. When a written summary would be useful, CJ may also provide an APC One-Concern Action Note; when provided, the target is within 2 working days after the session.
  • APC Home Implementation Programme (RM1,800): four sessions of 60 minutes each across approximately 6–8 weeks, usually scheduled once every two weeks across the implementation cycle. The programme includes a structured baseline, 1–2 agreed family priorities, a personalised APC Home Support Plan, implementation checks, review and adjustment across sessions, WhatsApp clarification throughout the active programme limited to the agreed plan, a final APC Maintenance Summary, and one check-in about a month after the final session. The maintenance check is included in the programme fee. WhatsApp clarification does not include crisis support or unlimited messaging.

If an agreed Home Implementation plan is not practical or is not producing useful information despite reasonable implementation, APC may keep, adjust, simplify, change direction, coordinate, or recommend another professional route. APC does not keep adding strategies solely to justify completing the programme.

Written Materials and Service Standards

When an APC One-Concern Action Note is useful and provided, it is targeted for delivery within 2 working days after the session. For the Home Implementation Programme, the initial Home Support Plan is targeted within 2 working days after Session 1, material plan updates are incorporated when the agreed approach changes, and the Maintenance Summary is targeted within 3 working days after Session 4. Clarification-message replies are targeted within 2 working days. These are service targets rather than guaranteed response times in emergencies; APC does not provide crisis or emergency support.

Bookings and Payment

For paid support, submit a request first. The Founder reviews suitability, capacity, and availability and gives individual written permission. Only after that permission should local clients pay by bank transfer to APC's Maybank business account or DuitNow QR, and international clients by bank transfer through Wise, using current details sent privately by the Founder. Payment proof is required through the private channel provided. CJ verifies payment before confirming the booking. Submitting a request, making payment, or sending proof does not create automatic booking confirmation. Prices are in Malaysian Ringgit (RM). The APC payment safety notice does not provide public payment instructions.

Calm Companion App

APC Calm Companion is a free, general educational tool for ordinary, non-emergency situations. It is not therapy, diagnosis, assessment, medical advice, crisis support, or a substitute for an individual professional review. It cannot determine why a situation is happening, and no action or outcome is guaranteed.

The app works without an account or profile. If you choose to add a name or nickname, it is stored only in that browser on that device until you remove it. Do not use a full legal name on a shared device.

Do not use the app when someone is in immediate danger, seriously injured, unable to breathe, at risk of running into danger, or when you cannot keep people safe. Contact the appropriate emergency service instead.

Feedback is voluntary and is used to improve the app. Do not submit names, diagnoses, schools, contact details, private information about a child, or requests for urgent help. APC may remove feedback that is unsafe, unlawful, abusive, unrelated, or contains information the form was not designed to collect.

APC may correct, update, pause, or withdraw the app when needed for safety, maintenance, or product improvement. Availability, installation, offline access, browser compatibility, and individual accessibility may vary by device.
