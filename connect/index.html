Understanding Escalation at Home

When a small moment becomes a big reaction, it can be hard to know what to notice or what to do next.

A short course is being developed to help parents look more closely at those moments and choose a manageable response at home.

Ask about the course

Not available to buy yet. No enrolment date or place is confirmed.

A closer look at the difficult moment.

The planned focus is understanding what happens before a reaction builds, considering demands and communication, and choosing one practical adjustment to review.

For example, getting ready to leave may involve several difficult steps. Looking at where the difficulty starts can give you a more useful question than simply asking how to stop the reaction.

Short enough to work through at your own pace.

The planned length is around 30 to 40 minutes. The content and supporting materials are still being developed.

Details will be shared before any enrolment opens. There is no payment or booking on this page.
