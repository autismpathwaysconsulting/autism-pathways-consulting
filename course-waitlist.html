Communication Course

Understand and connect with your child, at your own pace.

A self-paced online course for parents who want to read their child more clearly and respond in ways that build connection. The course is Hanen-backed, drawing on approaches that focus on everyday communication between parent and child.

Course waitlist · No payment on this page

  • Learn to notice what your child may be communicating through behaviour, sounds, and gestures.
  • Practical ways to slow down, tune in, and respond so daily moments feel more connected.
  • Self-paced, so you can work through it around family life.
  • Designed for parents, no clinical background needed.

This is parent education, not speech therapy. The course helps you understand and connect with your child at home. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists your child is already seeing.
