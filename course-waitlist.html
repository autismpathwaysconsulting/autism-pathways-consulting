Understanding Escalation at Home

A short course in development for parents who want to understand what may be building beneath difficult moments and choose a clearer next step.

Join the interest list ↓Explore all learning options ↗

Pre-launch

Interest first. Build second.

APC is gathering interest before finalising the first release. Joining the list does not create a booking or payment obligation.

What the course will help you understand

Look beneath the final behaviour.

Notice the build-up

Look at what may happen before escalation, including demands, communication difficulty, sensory load and regulation needs.

Make sense of the pattern

Move from reacting only to the final moment toward a clearer working understanding of what may be making the situation harder.

Choose one next step

Use a practical way to decide what to adjust first without trying to change everything at once.

Planned as parent education with examples and reflection prompts. It is not diagnosis, therapy or medical advice.
