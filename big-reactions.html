Free APC parent resource

When the reaction feels sudden, start with what happened around it.

The reaction is often the part we remember most. This short Quick Check helps you slow one moment down and notice what changed, what you saw or heard, what happened next, and what may be repeating.

Get the free Quick Check

General parent education only. Not diagnosis, therapy, treatment, or emergency support.

Start with one moment.

You do not need to solve the whole pattern today.

Does this feel familiar?

Use it for one moment that keeps repeating.

Stopping an activity becomes much harder than expected.

The transition may feel sudden even when you have asked more than once.

A small correction leads to a much bigger reaction.

The visible reaction may be the end of a sequence that started earlier.

A change in plan suddenly feels impossible.

What changed around the moment may be useful information.

The same request goes differently on different days.

The easier moments can be as informative as the difficult ones.

What the Quick Check does

Notice before you decide what to change.

One moment gives you a clue, not the answer. The aim is a clearer picture before choosing another strategy.

01What changed just before.
02What you actually saw or heard.
03What happened next, including what adults did.
04What happened afterwards.
05What seems different on easier days.

A calmer way forward

You do not need to work out the whole reason.

Use one copy for one specific moment. If a similar situation happens again, compare what stayed the same and what changed.
