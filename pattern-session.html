You don't need another list of tips

Tips are written for a general child. A pattern sheet starts with yours.

  • A hard moment happens, and you try something you read or were told.
  • It helps once, or it doesn't. Next week the moment looks a little different.
  • By evening you mostly remember the end. The part before it is easy to miss, and it is worth writing down.

How the session works

Bring a pen, and one moment from this month.

  1. 1

    Name the moment

    We look at before, during and after, using a made-up example.

  2. 2

    Fill in your sheet

    You work through your own sheet live, one section at a time.

  3. 3

    Choose one small thing

    You pick one change for this week, small enough to actually do.

What you take home: one finished sheet, and one small thing to try this week. Not a page of notes to sort out later.

What you'll learn to do

Skills you keep using on school mornings, outings and bedtime

  • Watch First. One quiet minute of noticing before you write anything.
  • Before, During, After. Write the moment in three parts, in your own words.
  • The Settle Check. Note whether your child settled alone or with your help, and what seemed to help.
  • One Small Thing. Choose a change small enough to try this week, and write down when.
  • The Share Sheet. Turn your sheet into one page for your child's teacher or therapist.

Included with your place

Everything you need to keep going after the hour

  • The full replay. Watch again at your own pace, and fill in a second sheet while you do.
  • The Share Sheet template. A one-page summary to bring to your child's teacher or therapist.
  • The Hard-Moment Card. A printable reminder of Watch First and your one small thing, for the fridge.
  • The day-7 check-in. Three short questions by email a week later: did you try it, what happened, and what would you change?

Before you join

Common questions

  • My child is not like your example.

    The example only shows how the sheet works. You fill it in from your own child's moments, and no two sheets look the same.

  • I've tried things before and nothing stuck.

    This starts from what is already happening at home, not from a new programme to follow.

  • Is this therapy?

    No. It is parent education and pattern review. If you have concerns about diagnosis, anxiety, sleep or health, a specialist should assess those.

  • How much is it?

    RM57 per parent. People on the Quick Check email list hear the date first, with no obligation to join.

  • Do I need the Pattern Finder Toolkit?

    No. The session uses its own sheet. The Toolkit is a separate printable set for comparing several moments at home.
