8. Quick reference


    

      September experiment protocol · DO NOT CHANGE MID-TEST
      

        
    
          
  • Primary snapshot: 72 hours after posting.
    • 
          
  • Secondary snapshot: 7 days after posting.
    • 
          
  • Compare only like with like: same platform, same format, same snapshot window.
    • 
          
  • Baseline: rolling median of comparable APC posts. The OS waits for at least 3 comparable prior records before labelling a post above/below baseline.
    • 
          
  • Replication: at least 2 independent hooks/angles in the same problem area before moving to explicit product validation.
    • 
          
  • Course gate: a paid workshop test must succeed before a full evergreen course is built.
    • 
          
  • Allowed mid-month changes: factual correction, software bug, cropping/readability issue, or genuine platform change only.
    •

Real parent moment → recognition → careful reframe → what to notice → practical next step → useful question.

      Evidence and safety boundaries
      

        
    
          
  • Do not treat one behaviour as proof of one cause.
    • 
          
  • Do not infer masking solely from school-home differences.
    • 
          
  • Medication stays with prescribers. APC can help parents observe and communicate.
    • 
          
  • Persistent sleep problems, nutritional concerns, pain, constipation, urinary symptoms, developmental regression or mental-health concerns may need professional assessment.
    • 
          
  • Use clinician/allied-health collaboration where the content crosses APC scope.
    • 
        

      

    


    

      When a topic earns a course
      

        
Do not build a course until enough gates are passed:

        
    
          
  • more than one post performs above baseline
    • 
          
  • parents describe the same problem repeatedly
    • 
          
  • parents explicitly ask for structured help
    • 
          
  • poll/waitlist confirms interest
    • 
          
  • paid workshop proves willingness to pay
