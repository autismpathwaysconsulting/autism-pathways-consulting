Programme interest

Review monthly. Contact new enquirers when capacity permits. Mark verified only after confirming the adult contact, one record per household and current interest. This does not confirm programme suitability.

Different emails can belong to one household. Keep one record and mark others duplicate. New and contacted records are unverified. Programme totals overlap and must not be added together.

Updates may go only to verified households with update permission, about their selected programmes. No automatic messages are sent here. For withdrawals, verify the request then delete the record. For corrected answers or consent, delete the verified old entry and invite a fresh submission. Do not retain child or health details in this tracker.
