What still needs preparing after scouting?

Once the venue and tasks are agreed, prepare the visit information sheet, participation consent, private support and emergency form, attendance and departure record, incident record, and separate optional media consent. These participant documents are not included in this scouting pack.

Confirm the applicable insurance, task requirements, emergency arrangements, costs and cancellation terms before offering a project place. The interest form and readiness checklist do not establish individual suitability.
