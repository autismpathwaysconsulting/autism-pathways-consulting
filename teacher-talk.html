About the programme.

Teacher Talk™ Training A

Encouraging Language Development in Early Childhood Settings

A Hanen programme supporting young children's communication through everyday interactions. For early-childhood educators working with children from birth to five, including children with typical development, language delays or learning a second language. Read the official programme description.

Completion documentation

Completion documentation follows programme requirements. This does not qualify participants as Hanen-certified providers.

Who leads the sessions?

CJ Lim, autism educator and founder of APC, with permission from The Hanen Centre to deliver Teacher Talk to educators.

Training a whole team?

For staff workshops, full-day training and video-based classroom support plans, see training for centres and schools.
