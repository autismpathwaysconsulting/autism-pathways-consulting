Common questions

Parents ask CJ these a lot

My child has no diagnosis yet. Can I still come?

Yes. You do not need a diagnosis. Many parents come while waiting for one. We work with what you can see right now.

Do you only work with autism? What about ADHD?

No - CJ works with autistic children, children with ADHD, and children with no diagnosis who are struggling. The approach fits any child whose behaviour or learning is confusing you.

Is the session in English?

Sessions are conducted in English. Bahasa Malaysia can be accommodated on request.

Do sessions happen online or in person?

APC Parent Support Programs are delivered online through structured parent support sessions, so families can receive guidance from anywhere in Malaysia or internationally.

How much does it cost?

APC Parent Support Programs currently start from RM1,000. The main 4-week Parent Support Program is RM1,800, and the most suitable option is usually confirmed after a Discovery Call or Parent Intake review.

My child is doing okay at home. Can you help with school problems?

Yes. Many parents come because their child is refusing schoolwork, falling behind, experiencing social difficulties, or being misunderstood by teachers or classmates. APC can help you understand what may be happening and what support may be needed.

My child is behind in Maths. Is that something you help with?

Yes. CJ has a strong background in Maths education for autistic children. Sometimes the subject is not the problem - the way it is being taught is. This can be worked on.

I am a teacher, not a parent. Can I still get help?

Yes. CJ works with teachers, shadow aides, and school staff. Visit the School Support page or WhatsApp CJ to discuss your situation.

Can I book more than one free Discovery Call?

Each family receives one free Discovery Call. This helps keep the free call focused, fair, and available for new families exploring APC for the first time.

If you have already had a Discovery Call and would like to clarify your next step, CJ may recommend a Progress Check Call if one focused follow-up is suitable.

What is the difference between a Discovery Call and a Progress Check Call?

A Discovery Call is a free 15-minute first step for new families. It helps CJ understand your main concern, check whether APC is the right fit, and recommend the most suitable next step.

A Progress Check Call is a paid 30-minute follow-up for families who have already had a Discovery Call. It is best used as one focused follow-up to clarify the next step. It is not ongoing support and does not include WhatsApp guidance.

If your concern requires repeated guidance, strategy planning, implementation support, or WhatsApp guidance, CJ may recommend the APC Parent Intake Form or a Parent Support Program instead.
