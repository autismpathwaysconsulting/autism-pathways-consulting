Common questions

Parents ask CJ these a lot

My child has no diagnosis yet. Can I still come?

Yes. You do not need a diagnosis. Many parents come while waiting for one. We work with what you can see right now.

Do you only work with autism? What about ADHD?

No - CJ works with autistic children, children with ADHD, and children with no diagnosis who are struggling. The approach fits any child whose behaviour or learning is confusing you.

Is the session in English?

Yes, sessions are in English. If you need Malay or a mix, message CJ on WhatsApp first to discuss.

Do sessions happen online or in person?

All parent coaching sessions are online via video call. This means you can join from anywhere in Malaysia.

How much does it cost?

A single session is RM350. A 3-session starter pack is RM950 (save RM100). A 6-session 3-month programme is RM1,800 (save RM300). See the Services page for full details.

My child is doing okay at home. Can you help with school problems?

Yes. Many parents come specifically because of school - refusing to go, falling behind, trouble with teachers or classmates. This is something CJ helps with a lot.

My child is behind in Maths. Is that something you help with?

Yes. CJ has a strong background in Maths education for autistic children. Sometimes the subject is not the problem - the way it is being taught is. This can be worked on.

I am a teacher, not a parent. Can I still get help?

Yes. CJ works with teachers, shadow aides, and school staff. Go to the Schools section or WhatsApp to discuss your situation.
