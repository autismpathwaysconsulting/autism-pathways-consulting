Questions

What’s included in Home Support?
  • 4 one-to-one sessions of 60 minutes
  • A one-page plan after every session
  • A weekly WhatsApp check-in
  • A check on day 3, and a final plan to keep
How much does it cost?

CJ confirms fit first, then shares fees privately. Nothing is paid on the call.

Is this therapy?

No. APC is parent education. It works with your child’s therapists, not instead of them.

What do I prepare for the call?

Nothing. Think of one moment that keeps repeating.
