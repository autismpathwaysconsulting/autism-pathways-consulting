Does this feel familiar?

Start with the moment that keeps repeating.

When the meltdown comes out of nowhere

Big reactions may be the end of a pattern that started much earlier.

When every routine becomes a battle

Mornings, homework, bedtime, or leaving the house keep taking more effort than expected.

When your child cannot tell you what they need

Silence, refusal, crying, pulling, or repeated phrases may all be communication.

What can change at home

From guessing in the moment to trying one clear next step.

For example, a difficult school morning might begin with too many instructions. A parent can notice where it gets stuck, show the next step in a familiar way, then see what still needs adjusting.

Illustrative example, not a client result. Every child and family is different.

How CJ works

Understand the moment, then choose a step you can try.

At home or in class, CJ starts by listening to what is happening, considers the task and communication demands, and helps identify a manageable response to try and review.
