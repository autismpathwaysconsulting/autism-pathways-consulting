Sound familiar?

You are not doing it wrong. The moment may be telling you something.

  • “Fine at school. Falls apart at home.”

    Holding it together all day can take a lot out of a child.

  • “I say stop. He stops. Tomorrow, again.”

    Stopping the moment is not the same as changing it.

  • “Everyone says be firm. Firm makes it worse.”

    Firm may stop the moment. It may not tell you why it keeps happening.

What a one-page plan looks like

Short enough for the fridge. Clear enough for a helper or grandparent.

Example plan

After school

What may be going on: holding it together all day, and home is where it feels safe to let it out.

Before
Snack and water in the car. No questions for the first 20 minutes. The same quiet corner at home.
During
Fewer words: one calm phrase, or a “home” picture card for a child who uses few words. Stay near.
After
A choice of two calm activities. Talk about the day later, in one sentence.
Helper
“No questions for the first 20 minutes, snack first.”
Example only. Your plan is built around your child, alongside your therapist’s ideas.

Questions

What does it cost?

The workshop is free. The 7-day course is RM98 for the first 20 parents, then RM197. Parent Coaching is RM4,997, or 3 payments of RM1,666 (RM7,997 with two home visits in the Klang Valley). For coaching, CJ checks fit on a free call first, then sends payment details privately. Nothing is paid on the call, and if it is not a fit after the first session, you get your money back in full.

We already have a therapist. What does this add?

Therapy sessions are usually time with your child. This is for you and the rest of the week at home: a plan for your hardest moment that you, your helper and grandparents can use, shaped around what your therapist already does. With your consent, CJ can share it with them.

What’s included in Parent Coaching?
  • 8 one-to-one online sessions of 60 minutes: weekly for the first 4, then every 2 weeks
  • One session just for your partner, helper or grandparent, and they can join any other session too
  • Visual supports and routine cards made for your child
  • A one-page plan after every session, and a final plan to keep
  • WhatsApp support between sessions, with replies the same working day
  • With your written consent, a short note to your child’s therapists and school
My child uses few or no words. Is this for us?

Yes. Everything starts from how your child communicates: words, signs, pictures, a device or behaviour.

Is this therapy?

No. APC is parent education. It works with your child’s therapists, not instead of them.

We live outside Malaysia

Everything is online, except home visits. Times are in Malaysia time (GMT+8). Prices are in ringgit, and your card or Wise converts them.
