About CJ

I spent over 10 years inside the classroom. Here's what I learned.

"Every behaviour is communication. Once you understand what your child is trying to say, everything changes."

I started my career as a shadow aide, sitting alongside autistic children in mainstream classrooms, day after day, figuring out what they needed to survive and thrive in an environment not built for them.

Over the next decade I worked with children and families in Petaling Jaya and Kuala Lumpur in classrooms, homes, and briefly in centres. I also supported children attending speech therapy and occupational therapy sessions, working alongside their therapists to carry strategies into everyday settings.

The problem was never that parents didn't care. They cared deeply. The problem was that nobody had explained the why behind the behaviour. Without understanding the why, it's impossible to respond effectively.

Why I started Autism Pathways Consulting

After completing my Master's in Special and Inclusive Education and earning my Hanen certifications, I realised I could support more families by helping parents and schools understand autistic behaviour, communication, emotional regulation, and learning needs through practical, evidence-based strategies.

The research is clear: parent involvement is one of the strongest predictors of child outcomes in early intervention. A landmark review in the Journal of Autism and Developmental Disorders found that parent-implemented interventions produced meaningful gains in communication, behaviour, and quality of life, especially when parents were coached with consistent, structured frameworks rather than generic tips.

Why this matters for you: The Hanen approach I use is built on decades of evidence that parents are the most powerful agents of change in their child's development. You don't need to be a therapist. You need to understand your child's communication and behaviour patterns, and know how to respond. That's exactly what these sessions teach.

APC is built on this: when parents and schools understand what a child may be communicating through behaviour, they can respond with more clarity, confidence, and compassion. My work focuses on practical strategies grounded in research and adapted for Malaysian families and school settings.

How I work

I don't give generic advice. Every session starts with understanding your specific situation: what behaviours you're seeing, when they happen, what's been tried, and what your family's day actually looks like. From there, I help you identify the possible why and build practical next steps around your child.

I also work directly in academic support, particularly Maths. Not every learning difficulty is a behaviour problem. Sometimes the way a subject is taught simply does not connect with how an autistic child processes information. Finding that access point is something I do regularly, either by guiding parents on how to support learning at home or working with the school directly.

For schools, learning centres, and kindergartens, visit the Schools section or WhatsApp me directly.

Ready to talk?

Each family receives one free 15-minute Discovery Call. This call is a first step to understand your main concern, check whether APC is the right fit, and recommend the most suitable next step. It is not a diagnosis, assessment, therapy session, coaching session, ongoing consultation, or detailed strategy session.

Already had a Discovery Call? CJ may recommend a Progress Check Call if one focused follow-up is suitable. For repeated guidance, strategy planning, implementation support, or WhatsApp guidance, the APC Parent Intake Form is the better next step.
