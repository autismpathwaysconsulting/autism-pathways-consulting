About CJ Lim

Practical support for the adults around autistic children.

For over 10 years, I have worked alongside autistic children, families and education teams. APC helps parents and educators make sense of what is happening, decide what matters first and translate evidence-informed ideas into practical next steps.

Parent supportSchools & educator PD

Experience

Grounded in education and real implementation.

My background is in special and inclusive education and long-term school-based support. That means I pay attention not only to what a strategy is called, but to whether it can actually be used during routines, lessons, transitions and difficult moments.

I founded Autism Pathways Consulting to give families and education teams a calmer, more structured way to interpret patterns, choose priorities and review what happens after a change is introduced.

For parents

APC parent support is APC's own educational and consultative service.

Parent sessions focus on understanding patterns, practical planning, implementation, observation and review. Hanen educator programmes are not repackaged into APC parent services.

For schools & educators

Professional development and implementation support are a separate service.

Schools can enquire about licensed Hanen early-childhood programmes or APC autism-specific consultation and professional learning.

Clear scope

APC is not a therapy clinic.

  • No diagnosis or medical services
  • No speech-language therapy
  • No psychology or crisis services
  • No claim that participants become licensed or certified providers unless the programme owner explicitly grants that status

How I work

Interpret → plan → implement → review.

Whether I am supporting a parent or an education team, the emphasis is on a manageable next step and what we learn after trying it.
