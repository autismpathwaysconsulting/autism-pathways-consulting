Payment safety

Payment details are not available on this website.

Do not make a payment using saved or previously published instructions. APC provides current payment details individually only after the Founder reviews suitability, capacity, and availability and gives permission to pay.

Paid support is subject to Founder suitability, capacity, and availability review.

International clients may be accepted for online support. After written permission to pay, local clients receive bank-transfer details for APC's Maybank business account or DuitNow QR details. International clients receive bank-transfer details through Wise. Payment details are always sent privately. This notice does not authorise a booking or payment.

Before paying

Check the current service details first.

Current Parent Home Support options, fees and booking steps are kept on the Parent Home Support page. Payment details are never published here and are sent privately only after CJ gives written permission to pay.

Manual control

The required sequence cannot be skipped.

  1. Submit a request.
  2. The Founder completes a suitability and capacity review.
  3. The Founder gives individual permission to pay and sends the current payment details privately.
  4. For local payments, use the bank-transfer details for APC's Maybank business account or DuitNow QR details provided by the Founder. For international payments, use the Wise bank-transfer details provided by the Founder.
  5. Send payment proof through the channel identified by the Founder.
  6. The Founder verifies the payment.
  7. The Founder confirms the booking and sends the session details.

Submitting payment proof does not automatically confirm a booking. APC provides parent education, interpretation, prioritisation, planning, implementation support, review, and adjustment. APC does not provide diagnosis, therapy, medical treatment, crisis work, unlimited messaging, or guaranteed outcomes.
