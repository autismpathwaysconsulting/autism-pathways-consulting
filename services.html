What does Hanen mean? Hanen training focuses on evidence-informed strategies that help adults support children’s communication through everyday interaction. For parents, this means the support is practical, relationship-based, and focused on real home routines.

About APC Autism Pathways Consulting is a Malaysia-based parent education and support service. APC provides structured parent guidance, not medical diagnosis, therapy, or clinical assessment.
Parent-focused session: APC support starts with the parent because parents are the ones managing the daily routines, transitions, communication breakdowns, and emotional moments at home. CJ does not need to meet your child first to begin helping you understand patterns and plan practical next steps. How APC Is Different

Therapy often focuses directly on the child during sessions.

APC focuses on helping parents:

  • Understand patterns underneath behaviour
  • Reduce overwhelm at home
  • Build clearer implementation systems
  • Adjust support strategies over time
  • Feel more confident and less alone
APC does not replace therapy. It complements the day-to-day implementation side that many parents struggle with.

The APC Pattern Mapping Method™

The APC Pattern Mapping Method™ helps parents move beyond reacting to behaviour and begin understanding the patterns underneath it.

This is what APC identifies together:

1
What triggers escalation or shutdown
2
What communication may be happening underneath the behaviour
3
What environmental changes reduce stress and overload
4
What sequence of support works best for THIS child
5
How to reduce parent-child power cycles over time

This becomes the foundation of the support plan, instead of relying on random strategies or generic advice.

What happens in an APC parent support session?

APC sessions are online parent support sessions with CJ Lim. Each session is usually 60 minutes and focuses on helping you understand your child’s behaviour, communication, regulation, routines, or learning challenges more clearly.

Sessions are conducted in English. Bahasa Malaysia can be accommodated on request.

Your child does not need to attend the session. We begin by understanding what you are seeing at home or school, then we map possible patterns and decide on practical strategies you can try during the week.

After the session, you will have a clearer idea of:

  • what may be happening underneath the behaviour
  • what to try next
  • what to observe
  • when to adjust the strategy

This is not therapy, diagnosis, or assessment. It is structured parent support to help you respond with more clarity and confidence.

What Happens After You Decide To Start

If a Parent Support Program feels like the right fit after a Discovery Call or Parent Intake review, the next step is a simple onboarding process so support feels clear, calm, and structured from the beginning.

1

Confirm Support Fit

We check the right support level, focus areas, timeline, and whether this feels manageable for your family.

2

Complete Parent Intake

You share key details about routines, communication, regulation, and family priorities so the support is grounded in your current needs.

3

Begin Weekly Support

We start with pattern mapping and then focus on practical strategies, troubleshooting, and clearer next steps each week.

4

Receive Summary & Next Steps

You receive a clear summary of key patterns, recommendations, and what to do next after this phase of support.

The goal is not to overwhelm parents with more information. The goal is to help families feel clearer, calmer, and more supported while implementing strategies at home.
