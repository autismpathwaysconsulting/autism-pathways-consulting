1:1 Parent Support & Strategy Session

Focused 45-minute online session · Malaysia and Southeast Asia

A focused support session for parents seeking practical, evidence-based guidance for communication, behaviour, emotional regulation, routines, school support, learning, and daily challenges.

This session is designed to help you move from confusion to clarity, so you can better understand what your child may be communicating and what to try next at home or in school.

Parent-focused session: CJ does not need to meet your child during this session. The session is based on the information you share, including your concerns, routines, examples from daily life, school feedback, therapy reports, and optional short video clips. The goal is not to diagnose or assess your child. The goal is to understand possible patterns and give you practical next steps.
This may help if your child
1

Melts down, shuts down, or becomes overwhelmed easily

We explore what may be happening underneath the behaviour instead of only focusing on stopping the behaviour.

2

Struggles to communicate needs clearly

We look at communication, emotional regulation, and environmental demands that may be affecting your child’s responses.

3

Has difficulties with school, routines, learning, or independence

We identify practical supports that fit your child, your family, and the school context.

What you can expect
1

You share what is happening

Tell CJ about your child’s current challenges, what you have tried, and what you are hoping to understand or improve.

2

CJ helps you understand the possible why

Behaviour, communication, and learning difficulties are explored through a practical, evidence-informed lens.

3

You leave with actionable next steps

You receive practical strategies and clearer direction that can be applied at home, in daily routines, or in school conversations.

Important note: This session is educational and supportive in nature. It does not replace medical, psychological, diagnostic, or emergency services. Recommendations are based on the information shared during the session.

Academic Support

Learning support for autistic and neurodivergent children

Not all learning difficulties are behaviour problems. Some children struggle with Maths, reading comprehension, or classroom tasks not because they won't, but because the way the content is being taught doesn't connect with how they think. CJ has a particular strength in Maths, and works with children to find the access point that makes sense for their specific learning profile.

Academic support is usually discussed through a 1:1 Parent Support & Strategy Session first, so CJ can understand your child’s learning profile before recommending next steps.

It can also be discussed as part of a school consulting engagement if the concern is happening mainly in the classroom.

School and Educator Consulting

Workshops, staff training and behaviour support · Limited spots

Autism Pathways Consulting works collaboratively with schools, learning centres, kindergartens, and small educational settings to better understand and support autistic students in real classroom environments. Support may include classroom strategies, behaviour and emotional regulation planning, student observation, teacher guidance, inclusion planning, and communication support strategies.

The goal is practical support that improves understanding, reduces stress, and creates more supportive learning environments for students.
