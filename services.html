Questions before booking

A few things parents often ask.

Do I need a First Step Call?

Book the free call if you are unsure or considering Home Support. When one focused parent concern and the scope are clear, you can submit an RM350 One-Concern Parent Session booking request without a First Step Call. Founder suitability and availability review and permission are required before payment.

Is APC therapy?

No. APC provides parent education and structured parent support, not diagnosis, treatment, or therapy.

Can APC work alongside my child's therapists?

Yes. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists your child is already seeing.

What happens after the request?

Submit a booking request first. The Founder reviews suitability and availability, then gives permission before payment. Only then should you pay RM350 by Maybank bank transfer or DuitNow QR and submit payment proof. The Founder verifies payment before confirming the booking manually and sending the Google Meet details. A request or payment is not automatic booking confirmation.

Which service should I choose?

Choose the free call if you are unsure, submit a One-Concern Parent Session request for one focused parent concern, or ask about Home Support if several concerns are connected.
