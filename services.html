How APC works

We do not start with “How do we stop the behaviour?” We start with “What is making this hard?”

  1. Notice what is happening

    Start with the moment that keeps repeating and what is happening around it.

  2. Understand the pattern

    Look at communication, regulation, routines, expectations, sensory load, and adult responses where relevant.

  3. Try one practical next step

    Choose something clear enough to use at home, then review what happens instead of adding more strategies at once.

Questions before booking

The questions parents often ask before paying.

Why pay for this when I can find autism strategies online?

You can find hundreds of strategies online. The difficult part is deciding which one fits your child in this situation, what to change first, and what to do when it does not work as expected. APC focuses on interpretation, prioritisation, and practical implementation.

Why would I need Home Support instead of one session?

The One-Concern Parent Session is for one focused concern when a practical next step is enough. Home Support is for concerns that need real-life implementation, review, and adjustment over approximately 6–8 weeks.

What if we have already tried lots of strategies?

That is often when another strategy list is least useful. APC looks at what you tried, when it was used, what happened, and what may have made it easier or harder to implement.

My child already has therapy. Is APC still relevant?

It can be. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists your child is already seeing. The focus is helping parents think through what is happening in ordinary home life and how recommendations fit the family's real routines.

Can you guarantee the behaviour will change?

No. APC does not guarantee a particular behaviour outcome. The aim is to help you understand the pattern more clearly, choose a practical next step, and review what actually happens rather than repeatedly guessing.

Do I need a First Step Call?

Book the free call if you are unsure or considering Home Support. When one focused parent concern and the scope are clear, you can submit an RM350 One-Concern Parent Session booking request without a First Step Call. Founder suitability and availability review and permission are required before payment.

Is APC therapy?

No. APC provides parent education and structured parent support, not diagnosis, treatment, or therapy.
