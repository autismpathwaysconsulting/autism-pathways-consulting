Parent support options

Choose the level of support that fits your family’s needs

APC Quick Clarity

Best for: Parents who want a clearer starting point.

Includes: 2 sessions, initial pattern review, practical next steps, and short WhatsApp clarification during the support period.

Most structured

APC Full Implementation

Best for: Parents ready to work through patterns and practise changes over several weeks.

Includes: 4 sessions, structured support planning, implementation guidance, and WhatsApp clarification during the support period.

APC Intensive

Best for: Families who need deeper support and more follow-up.

Includes: 6 sessions, extended support planning, implementation guidance, WhatsApp clarification during the support period, and a follow-up check-in.

What happens in a session

A simple, structured process

1

You share what is happening

Tell CJ about your child’s current challenges, what you have tried, and what you are hoping to understand.

2

We look for patterns

Together, we look at patterns across communication, regulation, routines, environment, and parent responses.

3

You leave with a practical next step

You will know what to observe, what to adjust, and what to try next at home or in daily routines.

What APC is not

A parent education and guidance service

APC does not diagnose, provide therapy, replace OT, speech therapy, psychology, or medical care, or provide crisis support. APC focuses on parent education, pattern review, support planning, and practical guidance.

Why parents choose APC

Calm, practical guidance that respects both the child and the parent

  • Autism-specific parent support
  • Practical strategies for real family routines
  • Emotionally safe, non-judgemental guidance
  • Structured support without overwhelming parents
  • A clear goal: helping more families understand what is happening and what to do next

CJ supports parents to understand their child more clearly and respond with more confidence.
