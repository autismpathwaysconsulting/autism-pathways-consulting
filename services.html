What happens in an APC parent support session?

APC sessions are online parent support sessions with CJ Lim. Each session is usually 60 minutes and focuses on helping you understand your child’s behaviour, communication, regulation, routines, or learning challenges more clearly.

Sessions are conducted in English. Bahasa Malaysia can be accommodated on request.

Your child does not need to attend the session. We begin by understanding what you are seeing at home or school, then we map possible patterns and decide on practical strategies you can try during the week.

After the session, you will have a clearer idea of:

  • what may be happening underneath the behaviour
  • what to try next
  • what to observe
  • when to adjust the strategy

This is not therapy, diagnosis, or assessment. It is structured parent support to help you respond with more clarity and confidence. APC does not replace occupational therapy, speech therapy, psychology, medical care, or diagnostic assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long before I see changes?

Most parents report noticing shifts within 2-3 weeks of implementing strategies consistently. Some changes happen sooner, others take longer depending on your child's needs.

Do I need to have my child diagnosed first?

A formal autism diagnosis helps, but it's not always required. If you're seeing signs of autism or your child is struggling with communication, regulation, or routines, APC can help you understand patterns regardless of diagnosis status.

Can I cancel or pause my program?

Life happens. If you need to pause or adjust your program timeline, message CJ to discuss options. We work with families, not against them.

What if APC isn't the right fit?

Payment is only arranged after CJ reviews your situation and confirms the fit. If during a Discovery Call or after your first session you realize it's not right, we can discuss alternatives or refer you to resources that might work better.

APC Coaching vs Therapy -- What's the Difference?

Therapy focuses on your child's emotions, diagnoses, and clinical treatment.

APC Coaching focuses on understanding your child's actual patterns and giving YOU the tools to manage daily life.

We're Not Therapy Because:

  • ✓ We don't diagnose or treat clinical conditions
  • ✓ We focus on practical daily strategies (not clinical treatment)
  • ✓ You become the expert, not dependent on weekly sessions

We Work Best When:

  • ✓ Your child is already diagnosed
  • ✓ You need practical strategies for right now
  • ✓ You want to understand your child deeply
  • ✓ You're ready to try a different approach

How APC Supports Families

Support that helps parents understand what to do at home.

Many parents receive advice from schools, therapists, or online resources, but still feel unsure how to apply it during real daily moments. APC helps bridge that gap with structured, practical parent guidance.

Communication Support

Helping parents use responsive interaction, visual supports, choices, modelling, waiting, and everyday routines to support communication.

Regulation & Behaviour Patterns

Helping parents identify what may be driving meltdowns, shutdowns, refusal, anxiety, or emotional escalation, then build calmer responses.

Routines & Independence

Helping families build practical routines for mornings, transitions, hygiene, homework, mealtimes, and daily independence.

Learning Readiness

Using structured teaching, movement-informed strategies, visual supports, and parent coaching to support attention, participation, and confidence.

Parent Carryover

Helping parents turn recommendations into simple home strategies that can be repeated, adjusted, and maintained.

Professional Coordination

Helping parents understand when to coordinate with school teams, OTs, SLTs, psychologists, doctors, or other relevant professionals.

Older Children & Adolescents

Support for parents navigating puberty, independence, and teenage changes.

APC also supports parents of older autistic children and adolescents who are navigating changes in regulation, hygiene, privacy, friendships, school expectations, and independence.

Puberty & Body Changes
