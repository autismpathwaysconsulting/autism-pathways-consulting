How APC works

We do not start with “How do we stop the behaviour?” We start with “What is making this hard?”

  1. Find the pattern

    Look at the moment, the demands, communication, regulation, routines, and adult responses around it.

  2. Choose what to change first

    A small practical next step is more useful than another long list of autism strategies.

  3. Review real life

    If ongoing support is needed, we use what actually happened at home to decide whether to continue, simplify, or adjust.

Questions before booking

The questions parents usually ask before paying.

Why pay for this when I can find autism strategies online?

You probably can find hundreds of strategies online. The difficult part is knowing which one makes sense for your child, in this situation, and what to change when it does not work as expected. APC focuses on interpretation, prioritisation, and practical implementation rather than giving you another generic strategy list.

We have already tried so many things. Why would this be different?

That is often when another strategy list is least useful. We look at what you tried, when you tried it, what happened around it, and why it may have worked in one situation but not another. The goal is not to claim a special technique. It is to understand the pattern well enough to make a better next decision.

My child already has therapy. Do we need APC too?

Not necessarily. APC does not replace therapy. APC works with parents on what happens across ordinary home life, including how recommendations fit routines, communication, regulation, and repeated difficult moments. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists your child is already seeing.

Can you guarantee the behaviour will change?

No. No responsible service can guarantee that a particular behaviour will disappear. What APC can do is help you understand the pattern more clearly, choose a practical next step, and adjust based on what actually happens rather than repeatedly guessing.

Why would I need the RM2,200 programme instead of the RM450 session?

The Home Clarity Session is for one recurring concern when a focused next step is enough. Home Support makes sense when the concern needs implementation over time. You try the plan, we review what happened, and we adjust it. If one session is enough, the programme should not be recommended.

I am already overwhelmed. Will this give me more homework?

The plan has to fit the family, not the other way around. APC aims to reduce overload by choosing the smallest useful change first. Short Parent Essentials resources are there when relevant, not as a four-week course you must complete.

Do I need a First Step Call?

Book the free call if you are unsure or considering Home Support. If one recurring concern is already clear, you can submit a Home Clarity Session request directly. The Founder reviews suitability and availability before payment.

Why are programme places limited?

APC works with a limited number of programme families at one time so there is enough capacity for preparation, review, and follow-through. If capacity is full, the next suitable start point will be discussed rather than creating artificial urgency.
