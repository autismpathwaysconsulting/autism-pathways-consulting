Questions before booking

A few things parents often ask.

Do I need a First Step Call?

Book the free call if you are unsure or considering Home Support. It is for fit and routing only. No public RM350 request or paid booking route is currently available. Founder suitability, capacity, and availability review and explicit written permission are required before payment.

Is APC therapy?

No. APC provides parent education and structured parent support, not diagnosis, treatment, or therapy.

Can APC work alongside my child's therapists?

Yes. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists your child is already seeing.

What happens before any payment?

For the RM350 session, there is no public paid booking route. If APC invites you to submit a booking request, the Founder reviews suitability, capacity, and availability and gives individual written permission. Only then should you pay using current Maybank bank transfer or DuitNow QR details sent privately and submit payment proof. The Founder verifies payment before confirming the booking manually. A request, payment, or proof is not automatic booking confirmation.

Are international services available?

No. International services and payments are currently unavailable.

Which service should I choose?

Use the free call only for fit and routing when suitability or scope is uncertain. Paid options are candidate services with no public request route and remain subject to individual Founder suitability, capacity, availability, and written permission before payment.

Can APC guarantee improvement?

No. APC supports parent understanding, planning, implementation, observation, review, and adjustment. APC does not guarantee behaviour, academic, developmental, communication, emotional, or family outcomes.
