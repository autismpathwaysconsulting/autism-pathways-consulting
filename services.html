Questions before booking

A few things parents often ask.

Do I need a First Step Call?

Book the free call if you are unsure or considering Home Support. It is a no-cost fit check for fit and routing only. Founder suitability, capacity, and availability review and explicit written permission are required before payment.

Is APC therapy?

No. APC provides parent education and structured parent support, not diagnosis, treatment, or therapy.

Can APC work alongside my child's therapists?

Yes. APC works with you, not instead of the therapists your child is already seeing.

What happens before any payment?

For the RM350 session, there is no public paid booking route. If APC invites you to submit a booking request, the Founder reviews suitability, capacity, and availability and gives individual written permission. Only then should local clients pay by DuitNow QR or bank transfer to APC's Maybank business account, and international clients by bank transfer through Wise, using details sent privately. The Founder verifies payment before confirming the booking manually. A request, payment, or proof is not automatic booking confirmation.

Are international services available?

Yes. APC may accept international clients for online parent support, subject to Founder suitability, capacity, and availability review. International payments are made by bank transfer through Wise after written permission to pay.

Which service should I choose?

Use the free call for a no-cost fit check when suitability or scope is uncertain. Paid support remains subject to individual Founder suitability, capacity, availability, and written permission before payment.

Can APC guarantee improvement?

No. APC supports parent understanding, planning, implementation, observation, review, and adjustment. APC does not guarantee behaviour, academic, developmental, communication, emotional, or family outcomes.
