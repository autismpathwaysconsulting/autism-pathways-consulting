Parent Coaching

1-on-1 online sessions · Malaysia and Southeast Asia

You want calmer mornings. Fewer meltdowns. A child who can tell you what is wrong. A school that actually understands him. These sessions help you get there by understanding what your child is really communicating, and building a plan that works for your specific situation.

What happens in each session
1

You describe what's happening

Share the specific behaviours, when they happen, what triggers them, what you've already tried. There are no wrong answers.

2

CJ explains the why

Every behaviour has a function. CJ explains what your child is communicating and why the current response isn't working yet.

3

You leave with a plan you can use today

Not generic tips. Specific strategies for your child, your home, your school situation. Parents often describe this as the first time someone gave them something that actually made sense.

4

You try it and come back (packages)

In the Starter Pack and 3-Month Programme, the next session reviews what happened, adjusts what needs adjusting, and goes deeper.

Why are sessions spaced 2 weeks apart? One week is too short to see whether a new strategy is working. Most behaviour changes take 10 to 14 days to become consistent. The 2-week gap gives you real time to try things at home, collect observations, and come back with actual results so we can build on what's working and fix what isn't.

Academic Support

Learning support for autistic and neurodivergent children

Not all learning difficulties are behaviour problems. Some children struggle with Maths, reading comprehension, or classroom tasks not because they won't, but because the way the content is being taught doesn't connect with how they think. CJ has a particular strength in Maths, and works with children to find the access point that makes sense for their specific learning profile.

This can be delivered as part of parent coaching (coaching you on how to support learning at home) or discussed directly as part of a school consulting engagement.
