Children and adults both learn

See how one activity could unfold.

Explore the four steps using a packing activity. The adult practises a strategy, then reflects with CJ on what helped.

01 / Before starting

Make the first step clear.

Let the child choose whether to pack an item or watch first. Agree how they can ask for help or a break.

“Would you like to put this in, or watch me first?”

Adult focus Notice the child's choice. Pick one realistic goal together.

02 / A simple demonstration

Show one step, then pause.

CJ demonstrates placing one item in the bag. The adult watches how a clear cue and waiting time can make the task easier to understand.

“One item goes in the bag. Then I wait.”

Adult focus Describe what you will try with your child.

03 / Supported practice

Try it. Notice what helped.

The child tries a short packing cycle with their adult. CJ observes, offers feedback and helps the adult adjust the support.

“What happened when you gave them more time?”

Adult focus Reflect on the support and the child's experience, including any wish to stop.

04 / Keep the useful part

Choose one thing to try again.

The child can show a preference through speech, gesture, AAC or another familiar way. The adult chooses one adjustment for the next activity.

“Next time, I will show one step and wait.”

Adult focus Carry one practical strategy into another everyday routine.

Asking for help, taking a break and choosing to stop are all part of participation.

Programme interest privacy notice · English

CJ Special and Inclusive Consultancy (003030209-T), trading as Autism Pathways Consulting, controls this interest list. Information comes from the adult completing the form. Required contact details, programme choices, age bands, location, availability and consent are used to plan possible programmes and respond to the enquiry. Phone, a request for a private support discussion, and permission for future updates are optional. Without required details we cannot process this form; you can still contact CJ directly.

Submitting the form is not consent to participate in an activity, be photographed, receive therapy or share information with a host. Only authorised APC staff have access. Cloudflare provides hosting, security checks and database storage and may process information outside Malaysia. We do not sell the list or share it with proposed hosts at this stage. Disclosure may occur where legally required.

We record consent wording version, submission date and contact-review status. Future programme updates are sent only if you opt in and only for selected programmes. No general newsletter is included. We review the list monthly and remove entries older than 180 days; the system also removes overdue entries when the list is next used. Records can be deleted sooner on a verified request. Contact CJ from your registered email for access, correction, withdrawal of updates or deletion: cjlim@autismpathwaysconsulting.com. We may verify the request before acting. Withdrawal does not affect any decision about suitability.

Notis privasi senarai minat program · Bahasa Melayu

CJ Special and Inclusive Consultancy (003030209-T), yang beroperasi sebagai Autism Pathways Consulting (APC), mengawal senarai minat ini. Maklumat diperoleh daripada orang dewasa yang melengkapkan borang. Butiran hubungan, pilihan program, lingkungan umur, lokasi, ketersediaan dan persetujuan yang diwajibkan digunakan untuk merancang program yang berpotensi dan menjawab pertanyaan. Nombor telefon, permintaan perbincangan peribadi tentang sokongan penyertaan dan kebenaran menerima kemas kini adalah pilihan. Tanpa butiran wajib, kami tidak dapat memproses borang ini; anda masih boleh menghubungi CJ secara langsung.

Penghantaran borang bukan persetujuan untuk menyertai aktiviti, dirakam gambar, menerima terapi atau berkongsi maklumat dengan pihak hos. Hanya kakitangan APC yang diberi kuasa mempunyai akses. Cloudflare menyediakan pengehosan, semakan keselamatan dan penyimpanan pangkalan data serta mungkin memproses maklumat di luar Malaysia. Kami tidak menjual senarai ini atau berkongsinya dengan bakal hos pada peringkat ini. Pendedahan boleh dibuat jika diwajibkan oleh undang-undang.

Kami merekodkan versi persetujuan, tarikh penghantaran dan status semakan hubungan. Kemas kini hanya dihantar jika anda memilihnya dan hanya untuk program yang dipilih. Ini tidak merangkumi surat berita umum. Kami menyemak senarai setiap bulan dan memadam entri yang melebihi 180 hari; sistem juga memadam entri tersebut apabila senarai digunakan semula. Rekod boleh dipadam lebih awal atas permintaan yang disahkan. Hubungi CJ menggunakan e-mel berdaftar untuk meminta akses, pembetulan, penarikan persetujuan kemas kini atau pemadaman: cjlim@autismpathwaysconsulting.com. Kami mungkin mengesahkan permintaan sebelum bertindak. Penarikan persetujuan tidak mempengaruhi keputusan tentang kesesuaian penyertaan.
