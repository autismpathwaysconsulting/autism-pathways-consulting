School & Educator Support

Practical autism-informed support for schools, learning centres, and educators supporting autistic students.

Register Interest Message CJ
🏫

Who This Is For

Support designed for education settings and the people who work with autistic students.

  • Schools
  • Learning centres
  • Kindergartens
  • Teachers
  • Shadow aides / PLAs
  • Small education teams
📌

What APC Can Support With

Practical guidance for teaching and supporting autistic students in real learning environments.

  • Understanding behaviour through an autism-informed lens
  • Emotional regulation and classroom overwhelm
  • Communication support
  • Transitions and routines
  • Classroom participation
  • Parent-school collaboration
  • Shadow aide / PLA guidance
  • Staff workshops and training
🧭

How Support May Look

Flexible consultation that fits your setting and the support your team needs.

1

Consultation call

Discuss the main concerns and what would help your setting most.

2

Classroom strategy review

Identify practical changes and supports for the learning environment.

3

Staff training session

Short, focused guidance for teachers and aides in your setting.

4

Educator guidance

Practical support for staff working directly with autistic children.

5

Parent-school meeting support

Help prepare for or contribute to constructive meetings with families.

6

Follow-up recommendations

Clear next steps so support can continue after the initial session.

Important Note

APC support is educational and consultative. It does not replace medical, psychological, diagnostic, or emergency services.
