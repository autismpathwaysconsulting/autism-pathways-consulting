Start with your team’s everyday challenges.

Understanding distress

Look beyond a behaviour label. Practise separating observations from explanations and consider communication, sensory demands and the environment.

Communication and participation

Review how instructions are given, how students can ask for help or a break, and what participation can look like for different learners.

Routines and transitions

Examine arrivals, changes of activity, task starts and recovery time. Identify adjustments your team can try and review.

Other discussion areas include parent-school collaboration, classroom overwhelm and guidance for shadow aides or personal learning assistants.

Choose how your team learns.

Focused staff workshop

A shared topic, practical examples and space to discuss how it applies to your classroom.

Small-team strategy discussion

A closer look at a recurring classroom situation and possible changes to adult responses or the environment.

Educator or aide guidance

Support with observation, classroom routines and communicating useful information to the wider team.

The scope, format, duration, fees and any follow-up are agreed with your school before booking. This enquiry does not reserve a date.

From your enquiry to the classroom.

  1. Tell CJ about your team. Include the setting, age group, team size and the concern you want to focus on.
  2. Agree a clear scope. Discuss suitability, the session focus, practical arrangements and fees.
  3. Work through relevant examples. Use de-identified situations and connect the discussion to what staff can do in everyday practice.
  4. Decide what to try next. Agree what the team will observe and how to review an adjustment. Any ongoing follow-up is confirmed separately.

APC provides educational training and consultation. These sessions are not diagnostic assessments, therapy, accredited certification or official training in a named third-party programme.

Before you enquire.

Who is this for?

Schools, learning centres, kindergartens, teachers, shadow aides, personal learning assistants and small education teams.

Can the topic be adapted to our school?

Share the main concern in your enquiry. CJ will discuss whether the topic fits APC's educational scope and what can reasonably be covered.

Can we discuss a particular student?

Start with a de-identified description. Do not send student reports, names or photographs through this public enquiry form. Any individual case discussion needs appropriate permission and an agreed scope.
