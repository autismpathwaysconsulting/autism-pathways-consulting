Schools & educator professional development

Professional development that reaches the classroom.

APC supports schools and early childhood teams with structured professional learning, practical application and follow-up. The aim is not simply to attend a workshop, but to help educators use what they learn in everyday educational settings.

Discuss your team's needsView programme options

Why this matters

Knowing the principle is different from applying it in a busy classroom.

APC's school work focuses on implementation: clarifying the problem, learning a structured approach, planning how it will be used, and reviewing what happens next.

IdentifyClarify the team's current challenge and what would make training useful.
LearnUse a defined professional-development programme or a clearly scoped APC workshop.
PractiseWork through examples, reflection and application rather than information alone.
ImplementTake agreed strategies back into the educational setting.
ReviewIdentify what worked, what was difficult and what support is needed next.

Licensed Hanen educator programmes

Structured early childhood professional development.

CJ holds current Hanen certification/licensing to provide the educator programmes below. These programmes support early language, social and literacy development across early childhood settings. They are not autism-specific programmes.

Early childhood educators

Teacher Talk™ Training Series

Three structured trainings designed to strengthen the quality of everyday educator-child interactions.

  • Three 7.5-hour trainings
  • 22.5 professional-development hours for the full series
  • In-person and online formats available within Hanen requirements
  • Training A is the foundation for the series
Intensive educator programme

Learning Language and Loving It™

An intensive Hanen programme for early childhood educators and teachers, combining interactive group learning with individual video feedback.

  • Focuses on social, language and literacy-supportive interaction
  • Includes practice, reflection and individual feedback
  • Available in the official in-person or online formats
  • Programme fidelity and participant limits follow Hanen requirements
Emergent literacy · ages 3–5

ABC and Beyond™

A Hanen programme for building educators' capacity to support emergent literacy through shared book reading and everyday interactions.

  • Full programme or Hanen-authorised modules
  • Interactive group learning
  • Individual video-feedback components where required
  • Supports educators to apply strategies independently with children

Credential note: CJ is certified to provide Learning Language and Loving It™ and ABC and Beyond™, and his Learning Language and Loving It™ certification includes a licence to deliver the Teacher Talk™ Training Series. Participant documentation will follow the requirements of the relevant programme. APC does not represent participants as Hanen-certified providers.

Autism-specific APC support

Practical support for implementation around autistic learners.

This work is separate from the Hanen programmes above. It draws on CJ's special and inclusive education background, school-based experience and additional professional learning, including SCERTS training.

Inclusion & participation

Review environmental demands, classroom participation and practical adjustments that may make learning more accessible.

Regulation & overwhelm

Look beyond behaviour alone and consider context, demands, regulation, predictability and adult responses.

Prompting & independence

Help teams think more deliberately about support, prompting and opportunities for the learner to do more independently.

Transitions & routines

Make recurring parts of the school day more predictable and easier to navigate.

Parent-school consistency

Clarify what is being observed across settings and support practical collaboration around agreed priorities.

Team implementation review

Follow up after training to identify what staff used, what got in the way and what needs adjustment.

Clear scope

What APC does and does not claim.

  • APC provides education, professional development and consultation.
  • APC does not provide speech-language therapy, diagnosis, psychology, medical or crisis services.
  • Hanen educator programmes are delivered within the relevant Hanen licence and programme requirements.
  • SCERTS training informs CJ's professional understanding; APC does not advertise an official SCERTS certification course.
  • APC does not currently advertise HRD Corp claimability, KPM accreditation or guaranteed child outcomes.
