APC implementation approach

Start with what needs to work differently.

Training is most useful when the team is clear about the problem, has something specific to practise, and gets a chance to review implementation. APC uses a simple five-part flow rather than assuming attendance alone creates change.

IdentifyDefine the recurring situation or staff-practice gap.
LearnChoose a structured programme or clearly scoped APC consultation.
PractiseWork through application, examples and planning.
ImplementUse the agreed approach in the real educational setting.
ReviewLook at what was used, what got in the way and what needs adjusting.

Licensed Hanen educator programmes

Structured early-childhood professional development.

These are official Hanen educator programmes that CJ is certified/licensed to provide. They focus on early interaction, language and literacy. They are not autism-specific programmes.

For early childhood settings and education teams
Entry point · 7.5 hours each

Teacher Talk™ Training Series

For early-childhood teams that want stronger everyday interaction, language-support and peer-support practices.

  • Three separate 7.5-hour trainings
  • 22.5 professional-development hours for the complete series
  • Training A is the foundation for B and C
  • Available in official in-person or online formats within Hanen requirements

Good fit when: the organisation wants structured staff professional development without the intensity of a full multi-session implementation programme.

Deeper implementation

Learning Language and Loving It™

For centres ready for a more intensive programme combining group learning with individual video feedback and practice in the educator's own setting.

  • Multi-session educator programme
  • Individual video-feedback component
  • Designed around applying strategies during everyday interactions
  • Programme format and participant limits follow Hanen requirements

Good fit when: leadership wants deeper practice change and can support the programme commitment.

Emergent literacy · ages 3–5

ABC and Beyond™

For teams focused on strengthening shared reading and emergent-literacy practice with young children.

  • Full programme or Hanen-authorised modules
  • Shared reading, vocabulary, story understanding and early literacy
  • Individual video feedback where required
  • Official programme/module requirements remain intact

Good fit when: the organisation's priority is early literacy rather than general autism training.

Credential clarity: CJ is certified to provide Learning Language and Loving It™ and ABC and Beyond™; Learning Language and Loving It™ certification also includes a licence to deliver the Teacher Talk™ Training Series. Attendance does not make participants Hanen-certified providers.

APC autism-specific support

When the problem is implementation around an autistic learner.

This is separate from the Hanen curriculum. APC's autism-specific work draws on CJ's special and inclusive education background, long-term school experience and additional professional learning.

Consultation and professional learning shaped around the setting
Access & participation

Inclusion and participation

Look at environmental demands, task access, participation and practical adjustments that may make the school day more workable.

Regulation & predictability

Regulation, routines and transitions

Consider context, sensory load, predictability, communication demands and adult responses instead of treating behaviour in isolation.

Support without dependence

Prompting and independence

Help teams identify where support is genuinely needed, where adults can wait, and how to reduce unnecessary dependence on prompts.

Consistency across adults

Team and parent-school alignment

Clarify what different adults are seeing, agree practical priorities and improve consistency between classroom, support staff and family.
