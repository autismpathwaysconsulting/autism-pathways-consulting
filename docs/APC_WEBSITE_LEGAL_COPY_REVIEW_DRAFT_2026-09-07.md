## Terms and Conditions candidate copy

### Status and application

These proposed service terms apply only after APC supplies them before payment and records the parent or purchaser's affirmative acceptance. They do not change an existing client's agreement retrospectively. Paid support remains subject to individual Founder suitability, capacity, and availability review.

### APC's role

APC provides parent education, pattern review, and structured parent support. It is not therapy, diagnosis, clinical treatment, medical advice, emergency support, or a substitute for professionals already supporting the child. APC does not guarantee a particular result.

### Services

- Free 15-Min First Step Call: a fit-and-routing conversation. It is not a consultation, assessment, diagnosis, therapy, or advice session.
- One-Concern Parent Session, RM350: one 45-minute online session for one repeated concern, with focused pattern review, one practical next step, and a concise written next-step summary.
- APC Home Support Programme, RM1,800: four 60-minute online sessions, normally delivered about once every two weeks over approximately six to eight weeks. It includes a personalised Home Support Plan, implementation review and adjustment, bounded weekly parent updates and brief WhatsApp clarification about the agreed plan during the active programme, and a final written plan or summary. It does not include an additional post-programme check-in, crisis support, unrelated case work, or unlimited messaging.

### Information before payment and acceptance

Before payment, APC must provide the service provider's identity and operating address, contact details, service description, full price and any other cost, payment method, expected service timing, Terms, Cancellation Policy, Privacy Policy, and Parent Support Agreement version. APC must record the parent or purchaser's identity, service, version, date, time, and affirmative acceptance and give the parent a durable copy. Payment does not replace acceptance of an unstated term.

### Home Support timing

APC and the parent should arrange provisional dates at onboarding and should aim to complete the programme within 12 weeks after the first paid session. The 12-week point is a review point, not automatic expiry. A pause or extension may be agreed in writing for illness, family circumstances, safety needs, APC availability, or another material interruption, with a restart or review date.

If a programme remains unfinished, APC will confirm the recorded sessions delivered and remaining and ask the parent to book, agree a dated pause, or discuss early closure. APC will not automatically forfeit a paid session or decide a refund solely because the intended period has passed.

### Cancellations, early closure, and refunds

The Cancellation Policy forms part of the accepted agreement. No system makes an automatic session-use, programme-closure, credit, refund, or consumer-rights decision. Nothing in these terms excludes or limits a right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under Malaysian law.

### Parent responsibilities

The parent decides whether a suggested step is safe and practical for the family and remains responsible for supervision, medical decisions, and obtaining emergency or qualified professional help when needed. The parent agrees to provide reasonably accurate, relevant information and to tell APC when circumstances materially change.

### Personal information

APC handles enquiry, booking, payment-reference, intake, session, action-plan, and communication records as described in the Privacy Policy. A person who shares information about a child or another person confirms that they are authorised to do so and agrees to share only information reasonably needed for the service.

### Materials

Session summaries, action plans, and templates are educational materials for the purchasing household. They may be shared with the family's own professionals for that child's support. They may not be sold, publicly distributed, or commercially reproduced without written permission.

### Liability and mandatory rights

APC cannot guarantee a specific outcome. Any limitation or exclusion applies only to the fullest extent permitted by law. Nothing in the terms excludes liability or a consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited. The legal effect of any proposed fee cap requires qualified Malaysian legal approval before use.

## Cancellation and Refund Policy candidate copy

### Free calls

If a parent needs to change a free call, they may use the options in the calendar invitation or contact APC. No cancellation fee applies. Repeated missed calls may be reviewed manually before another call is offered.

### Paid appointments

- At least 24 hours' notice: the appointment may be rescheduled without losing the booked session, subject to available dates.
- Less than 24 hours' notice or no-show: the appointment normally counts as used, subject to exceptional-circumstances review, the accepted agreement, and applicable legal rights.
- Late arrival: the scheduled end time does not normally change.
- APC cancellation: the parent does not lose the session and APC will offer a replacement date.

APC makes exception decisions manually. The booking system must not automatically forfeit a session, deny a refund, or make a final consumer-rights decision.

### Programme timing and inactivity

The four-session Home Support Programme is normally delivered about once every two weeks over approximately six to eight weeks. The parties should aim to complete it within 12 weeks after the first paid session. The 12-week point is a review point, not automatic expiry.

APC may send a written reactivation message with a seven-day response request and one reminder. The parent may book, agree a dated pause, or discuss early closure. No paid session is automatically forfeited and no refund is automatically decided because the parent does not respond or because the intended period has passed. Active between-session clarification may be paused while the programme is dormant.

### Early closure, unused sessions, credits, and refunds

Any financial outcome must consider the work delivered, unused paid sessions, the reason for closure, exceptional circumstances, the accepted agreement, and applicable Malaysian law. APC will give a written decision. No blanket no-refund rule is proposed.

### Existing clients and legal rights

A new completion window or consequence is not applied retrospectively. For a programme that began before final wording was accepted, APC will agree a fair recovery timetable in writing and preserve the recorded sessions delivered and remaining. Nothing in this policy excludes or limits rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited under Malaysian law.

## Privacy Policy candidate copy

### Information collected

- Name, email address, and telephone number for enquiries, bookings, and communications.
- Appointment dates, attendance, service status, and communication history.
- Information about the child, household, routines, communication, regulation, and family situation that the parent chooses to share for parent support.
- Intake and template answers, session notes, facilitator notes, parent summaries, action plans, resources supplied, and completion records.
- Payment references and records required to verify a transaction.
- Newsletter information only where the person opts in.

APC does not ask a child to provide information directly. Child health or diagnosis information may be sensitive personal data. APC will request only information reasonably needed and will obtain recorded express consent where required. A person sharing information about a child or another person must be authorised to do so.

### Purposes

APC uses information to respond to enquiries, assess service fit, arrange and deliver support, prepare session summaries and action plans, communicate about the engagement, verify payments, maintain service and legal records, respond to safety concerns where appropriate, and send marketing only where the person opted in.

APC does not sell client personal information. Disclosure is limited to service providers used for stated purposes, people the parent authorises, and disclosures required or permitted by law.

### Confidentiality

APC treats intake, session, and programme information as confidential and restricts access to authorised use. APC may disclose the minimum necessary information where required or permitted by law, where the parent authorises disclosure, or where disclosure is reasonably necessary to respond to a serious and imminent safety concern. This does not promise legal privilege or absolute confidentiality.

### Providers and client-record systems

The final notice must identify each provider actually used and its purpose. The current inventory to verify includes Cal.com, Google Workspace and private Google Drive, Zoho Mail, WhatsApp, Maybank, Wise, Brevo, Gumroad, Cloudflare hosting, Cloudflare Turnstile, Cloudflare D1, and every generative AI workspace used for APC work.

### Cross-border processing

Before APC transfers identifiable personal information outside Malaysia, APC must identify an applicable transfer condition, inform the parent of the relevant class of recipient and purpose, take reasonable security precautions, and keep the required transfer record. Where consent is relied on, it must be recorded and maintained.

### AI-assisted drafting

An approved generative AI workspace may help organise notes or draft parent-facing materials only after the required privacy and vendor controls are complete. AI must not make clinical, safety, eligibility, refund, or legal decisions. CJ must review every output before use.

### Retention and security

APC must approve a record-specific retention and deletion schedule before identifiable Practice Console cloud storage is activated. Access should be restricted, accounts protected with multi-factor authentication where available, exports controlled, backups and deletions governed, and access or deletion actions recorded.

### Data incidents

APC must maintain an incident log and promptly assess suspected loss, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Where notification duties apply, APC must follow the current Personal Data Protection Commissioner requirements for notification to the Commissioner and affected people.

### Individual rights

The final notice must explain how a person may request access or correction, withdraw consent where applicable, stop direct marketing, ask about retention, or request deletion where appropriate and legally available, together with APC's response channel.

### No automated client decisions

APC does not authorise an app or AI system to make final decisions about service suitability, programme closure, session use, refunds, safeguarding, or a child's needs. Those decisions require human review.
