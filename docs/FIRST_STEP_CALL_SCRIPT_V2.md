# APC Free 15-Minute First Step Call — Script V2

Status: Internal operating script
Purpose: fit + routing only. This is not a consultation, assessment, diagnosis, therapy session, or personalised strategy session.

## Booking form minimum context

Cal.com should collect only the information needed to avoid starting the 15-minute call from zero.

Required fields:
1. Name
2. Phone number
3. Email
4. Child age (age only, not date of birth)
5. “What would you like help deciding on this call?”

Recommended helper text for question 5:

> In 1–2 sentences, tell me what you are unsure about. Please do not include your child’s name, school, diagnosis, medical history or other identifying details.

Do not turn the free-call form into a clinical intake.

## Outcome promise

By the end of the call, the parent should know:
- whether the concern is within APC’s scope,
- which APC support route, if any, fits best,
- whether another type of professional route may be more appropriate,
- what the next step is if they choose to continue.

The call should NOT attempt to determine:
- why a child is behaving a certain way,
- a detailed working hypothesis,
- personalised strategies,
- diagnosis,
- a treatment/intervention plan.

Those require paid support or another professional route.

## 0:00–1:00 — Frame the call

Suggested wording:

> “Thanks for booking. This call is to work out whether APC is the right fit and which next step makes sense. I won’t try to solve the concern in 15 minutes, but by the end I want you to know whether I can appropriately help, which route fits, or whether a different professional would make more sense.”

## 1:00–4:00 — Fill the missing context

If the booking answer is useful, start there:

> “You mentioned that you are trying to decide [briefly paraphrase their answer]. What made you book now?”

Then confirm:
- “How old is your child?”
- “What are you hoping to decide by the end of this call?”

If the optional field was blank:

> “That’s okay. In one or two sentences, what made you book the call today?”

Do not take a full developmental history.

## 4:00–8:00 — Clarify the type of support needed

Use only enough questions to route safely:

> “Is this mainly one repeated concern, or are several areas interacting?”

> “Has this been happening for a while, or has something changed recently?”

> “Are you already working with any relevant professionals around this concern?”

Safety/scope check where relevant:

> “Is there anything urgent involving immediate safety, medical needs, crisis, or a concern that requires a regulated professional?”

If yes, route/referral should take priority.

## 8:00–12:00 — Route

### Route A — RM350 One-Concern Parent Session
Use when:
- there is one focused repeated concern,
- parent wants to understand the pattern and choose a practical next step,
- issue is within APC’s educational/consultative scope,
- it does not clearly require multi-week implementation support.

Suggested wording:

> “This sounds focused enough for the One-Concern Parent Session. That is where we can slow the situation down properly, look at what may be contributing to it, and leave with a practical plan to test.”

### Route B — RM1,800 Home Support Programme
Use when:
- several factors interact,
- the family has already tried advice that has not held,
- implementation needs review/adjustment over time,
- a plan needs several cycles of try → observe → adjust,
- concern affects several routines or settings.

Suggested wording:

> “I don’t think one conversation would do this justice. The Home Support Programme makes more sense because we can establish a starting point, try a manageable change, review what actually happened, and adjust over several weeks.”

### Route C — Another professional / existing team
Use when:
- diagnosis/assessment is the main need,
- speech-language therapy is required,
- OT/medical/psychological/crisis expertise is needed,
- current professionals are better placed to address the request.

Suggested wording:

> “I don’t think APC should be the main service for this part of the concern. The more appropriate next step is [type of professional/current team]. I would rather be clear about that than sell you a session that is not the right fit.”

### Route D — No paid support needed now
Use when:
- parent already has an adequate plan/team,
- question is too general for paid work,
- a free resource is sufficient,
- waiting/observation is reasonable and safe.

## 12:00–14:00 — Explain the route

Keep this short. Explain:
- why the route fits,
- what it would and would not cover,
- no guaranteed outcomes,
- paid support is still subject to availability/suitability.

Do not start giving the paid-session strategy here.

## 14:00–15:00 — Close

Suggested wording:

> “So the clearest next step from what you’ve told me is [route]. You don’t need to decide on this call. If you want to continue, I’ll send or point you to the next step. If not, you still leave knowing which direction makes the most sense.”

## If a parent asks for strategy during the free call

Suggested wording:

> “That is exactly the kind of question I would want to explore properly rather than give you a quick answer without enough context. Today I can help you decide whether the RM350 session, the longer Home Support route, or another professional is the right place to work on it.”

## Practice Console use

Continue using the Practice Console for the paid-client journey and operational record.

For the free call itself:
- use this script as the call checklist,
- record only the minimum routing outcome needed for follow-up,
- do not create a detailed client formulation from a 15-minute fit call,
- if the parent proceeds, collect proper intake information in the paid-service workflow.

Future system improvement: add a distinct `FIT_CALL` / `ROUTING` stage to the Practice Console rather than forcing a free enquiry into the RM350 or RM1,800 journey before fit is established.

## Quality check after each call

Ask yourself:
- Did the parent leave knowing the next route?
- Did I avoid solving the paid concern for free?
- Did I stay within educational/consultative scope?
- Did I avoid unnecessary identifiable/sensitive information?
- Did I refer out when needed?
- Did I avoid pressure-selling?
