# APC Parent Offer Price Increase Plan

Status: Internal pricing strategy. Do not publish until CJ approves deliverables, transition policy and website copy.

## 1. Core decision

Do not increase price by simply charging more for the same 45-minute / four-session structure.

Increase price by making the service more defined, more implementation-focused and more tangible while remaining within APC's educational/consultative scope.

## 2. Market context

Current examples are not direct equivalents but help calibrate the market:

- Lucent Wellbeing lists parent consultation/coaching at RM450 for a 90-minute specialist session, RM1,220 for three 90-minute sessions and RM2,330 for six 90-minute sessions.
- Singapore provider Nightingale Pediatrics lists 1-to-1 parent coaching at S$300 per 50 minutes.
- Morning Star Singapore lists an Incredible Years autism parenting programme at S$988 per family for six 2-hour group sessions plus two 1-hour individual coaching sessions.

These comparators do NOT prove the correct APC price. They show that APC's current RM350 / 45-minute session is already premium on a time basis and that higher pricing must be justified through specialisation, preparation, written outputs, implementation support and convenience.

## 3. Recommended next-price test

### Option A - safer first increase

**APC One-Concern Parent Session: RM450**
- 60 minutes live online
- short structured intake reviewed before session
- one working explanation / hypothesis
- one priority
- 1–2 practical changes to test
- one-page APC One-Concern Action Note
- one clarification message within 7 days, strictly limited to the agreed concern/plan

**APC Home Implementation Programme: RM2,400**
- four 60-minute sessions across approximately 6–8 weeks
- baseline intake and priority-setting before Session 1
- personalised Home Support Plan
- bounded WhatsApp clarification during the active programme
- brief between-session implementation check-ins
- plan reviewed/updated across sessions
- final maintenance summary
- approximately 20–30 minute maintenance check about one month after Session 4

This is the recommended starting price test because it materially strengthens the offer while remaining closer to current Malaysian willingness-to-pay signals.

### Option B - stronger premium positioning after proof

After several successful clients / strong demand / waiting list:

**One-Concern Parent Session: RM500**

**Home Implementation Programme: RM2,600–RM2,800**

Only move here once APC has evidence of:
- consistent demand,
- low price resistance,
- strong client feedback,
- repeat/referral behaviour,
- limited capacity,
- clear deliverables and operational efficiency.

## 4. Why not raise immediately to RM600 / RM3,000+

Potential risks:
- local parent-coaching comparators are lower,
- APC is intentionally not a regulated clinical service,
- social proof for the new packaging is still limited,
- the Home Support structure is still being standardised,
- aggressive price increases before outcome/documentation proof could increase trust friction.

Premium pricing can become justified later through evidence, scarcity, implementation quality and referrals rather than claims of clinical authority.

## 5. What the higher price pays for

### RM450 One-Concern
The buyer is no longer purchasing only live time. The service includes:
1. intake review before the meeting,
2. 60-minute focused consultation,
3. pattern/hypothesis synthesis,
4. decision on 1–2 changes to test,
5. written Action Note,
6. bounded clarification window.

### RM2,400 Home Implementation
The buyer is purchasing:
1. baseline preparation,
2. four live sessions,
3. implementation guidance between sessions,
4. Home Support Plan development and updates,
5. review of parent-reported data/observations,
6. final maintenance plan,
7. post-programme maintenance check.

The price should not be framed as 'RM600 per session'. It is a programme fee for an implementation cycle.

## 6. Keep Hanen / SCERTS boundaries clear

The parent-service prices do NOT include delivery of:
- Learning Language and Loving It™,
- Teacher Talk™,
- ABC and Beyond™,
- an official SCERTS programme.

CJ's professional learning may inform his general educational judgement where appropriate, but proprietary curricula/materials must not be repackaged into APC parent services unless the programme licence explicitly allows it.

## 7. Recommended names

Keep the offer ladder simple:

**Free 15-Min First Step Call**
Fit + routing.

**APC One-Concern Parent Session**
Focused clarity + Action Note.

**APC Home Implementation Programme**
Guided implementation + review + adjustment + maintenance.

Optional later:

**APC Progress Review**
A focused 60-minute review for former clients at approximately 3 months, a school transition, puberty/adolescence change, or another meaningful transition.

Do not launch the Progress Review until existing clients create real demand.

## 8. Transition policy when price changes

Recommended:
- existing fully paid clients keep the price/package they purchased,
- existing active RM1,800 clients are not repriced mid-programme,
- new enquiries after the public effective date receive the new pricing,
- quotations already issued remain valid for a clearly stated validity period,
- no fake urgency or artificial 'normal price' discounting.

## 9. What to validate before publishing

- [ ] CJ approves RM450 / RM2,400 as first test prices.
- [ ] Client-facing Action Note is ready.
- [ ] Home Support Plan / maintenance summary are ready.
- [ ] Clarification boundaries are written.
- [ ] Website clearly distinguishes one-off consultation vs implementation programme.
- [ ] Terms/payment/cancellation wording is updated.
- [ ] Existing-client transition policy is decided.
- [ ] Practice Console supports the revised workflow.
- [ ] At least 3–5 qualified buyer conversations are logged after the increase.

## 10. Success measures

Monitor after launch:
- enquiry-to-paid conversion,
- price objections,
- percentage choosing RM450 vs RM2,400,
- delivery hours per client,
- effective revenue per practitioner hour,
- referral rate,
- repeat review demand,
- client completion rate,
- amount of between-session messaging,
- whether deliverables are actually used by parents.

Do not raise again until these data show the current pricing is under-valuing capacity or demand.
