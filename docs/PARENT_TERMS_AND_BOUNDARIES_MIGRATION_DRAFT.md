# APC Parent Services — Terms & Boundaries Migration Draft

Status: Internal draft. Not legal advice. Do not publish until CJ approves the new price/effective date and the live terms/cancellation pages are reconciled.

## 1. Services covered

### Free 15-Min First Step Call
- fit and routing only,
- no personalised strategy plan,
- no diagnosis/assessment/therapy,
- no obligation to accept a paid case.

### APC One-Concern Parent Session
Working Malaysia price: RM450
Working Singapore price: S$200

Includes:
- structured intake review,
- one 60-minute online consultation,
- one bounded concern,
- Action Note,
- one clarification message within 7 calendar days.

### APC Home Implementation Programme
Working Malaysia price: RM2,400
Working Singapore price: S$980

Includes:
- baseline intake,
- four 60-minute online sessions across approximately 6–8 weeks,
- Home Support Plan,
- bounded between-session clarification/check-ins,
- plan review and adjustment,
- Maintenance Summary,
- 20–30 minute maintenance check approximately one month after Session 4.

## 2. Suitability / acceptance

Paid support remains subject to:
- scope fit,
- CJ's capacity,
- availability,
- safeguarding/privacy considerations,
- whether another professional is more appropriate.

A request or free call does not guarantee acceptance into paid support.

## 3. Payment sequence

Keep the current manual-control principle:
1. request / fit review,
2. CJ approves the service and price in writing,
3. payment details are sent privately,
4. client pays,
5. client submits proof where required,
6. APC verifies payment,
7. booking/programme is confirmed.

Do not publish bank/Wise details openly.

## 4. Payment options

### One-Concern
Recommended default:
- full payment before the session.

### Home Implementation
Recommended default:
- full payment before Session 1.

Potential access option if approved:
- two instalments,
- first before Session 1,
- second before Session 3.

The total programme fee does not reduce because instalments are used.

## 5. Existing-client transition

When new pricing becomes effective:
- active paid clients keep the agreed package and price,
- active RM1,800 clients are not repriced,
- previously issued written quotes remain valid for the period stated in the quote,
- new price applies to new approvals/quotes after the effective date.

Avoid retroactive repricing.

## 6. Rescheduling / cancellation principles

The live cancellation policy must be checked and then updated consistently.

Recommended commercial rules for CJ/legal review:

### One-Concern
- allow one reasonable reschedule with required notice,
- late cancellation/no-show may result in the session fee being forfeited or a rebooking fee,
- emergency/illness handled reasonably at CJ's discretion.

### Home Implementation
- programme fee reserves a multi-session implementation cycle, not four unrelated appointments,
- missed/rescheduled sessions must remain within a reasonable programme window,
- long pauses may require re-baselining or a new agreement,
- unused sessions are not automatically converted to unrelated concerns,
- if APC ends the service for scope/safety reasons, undelivered portions should be handled according to the written terms.

Exact notice periods/refund percentages require CJ approval and, if desired, legal review.

## 7. Messaging boundary

### One-Concern
One clarification message within 7 calendar days.

A clarification:
- relates directly to the documented concern/plan,
- is not a new concern,
- is not a second consultation,
- is not crisis support.

### Home Implementation
Bounded WhatsApp clarification during the active programme.

Recommended target standard:
- messages may be sent asynchronously,
- CJ aims to reply within 2 working days,
- no guaranteed same-day response,
- no emergency/crisis support,
- no unlimited message volume,
- substantial new concern may require a scheduled session/new service.

## 8. Working-day definition

Recommended wording:

> "Working days means Monday to Friday excluding Malaysian public holidays and days when APC has given advance notice of closure."

If Singapore clients are served, do not promise Singapore public-holiday response rules unless operationally intended.

## 9. Documentation turnaround

Recommended standards:
- One-Concern Action Note: within 2 working days,
- initial Home Support Plan: within 2 working days after Session 1,
- material updates: after review sessions where the plan changes,
- Maintenance Summary: within 3 working days after Session 4.

These are service standards, not guarantees if exceptional circumstances are communicated.

## 10. Scope / non-clinical boundary

Use consistent language across website, terms and client documents:

APC provides:
- parent education,
- pattern review,
- practical support planning,
- implementation guidance,
- review and adjustment.

APC does not provide:
- diagnosis,
- speech-language therapy,
- occupational therapy,
- psychological treatment,
- medical care,
- crisis/emergency services.

## 11. Third-party programmes

Parent packages do not include delivery of:
- Learning Language and Loving It™,
- Teacher Talk™,
- ABC and Beyond™,
- an official SCERTS programme.

CJ's professional learning may inform judgement but proprietary third-party curricula are not repackaged into APC parent services.

## 12. Shareable Support Summary

If offered:
- parent must request/approve sharing,
- summary remains non-diagnostic,
- it documents the family's current priority and support plan,
- it does not direct another regulated professional's practice,
- APC does not send it to third parties without parent consent.

## 13. Supported-rate access

If CJ approves a supported-rate pathway:
- availability is limited,
- service quality/scope remains the same,
- supported rate is not a negotiation entitlement,
- no unnecessary financial evidence is collected,
- supported places may be paused when the capped allocation is full.

## 14. Safety / referral

APC may recommend another professional or stop/redirect work when:
- the concern is outside APC scope,
- urgent safety/medical/mental-health assessment is required,
- information suggests a regulated clinical assessment is necessary,
- continuing would create safeguarding/privacy risk.

## 15. No guaranteed outcomes

Use explicit language:

> "APC supports planning, implementation and review. APC cannot guarantee a particular developmental, behavioural, communication, academic or family outcome."
