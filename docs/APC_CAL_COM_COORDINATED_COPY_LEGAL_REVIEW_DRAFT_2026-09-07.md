## Free 15-Min First Step Call

> This free 15-minute call checks fit and the appropriate next step. It is not a consultation, assessment, diagnosis, therapy, advice session, or emergency support. If you need to change the call, please use the rescheduling option in your calendar invitation. No cancellation fee applies. Repeated missed calls may be reviewed manually before another call is offered.

## Paid appointment short notice

> Payments are not refunded for parent change of mind, cancellation, unavailability, or non-attendance. A paid appointment may be rescheduled once without losing the booked session when at least 24 hours' notice is provided, subject to available dates. Changes with less than 24 hours' notice or missed appointments normally count as used, subject to the recorded exceptional-circumstances review, the agreement you accepted, and applicable legal rights. Late arrival does not normally extend the scheduled end time. If APC cannot deliver paid work, or if the service fails to meet a non-excludable legal guarantee, the remedies required by Malaysian law still apply. Please read and accept APC's Terms, Cancellation and Refund Policy, Privacy Policy, and Parent Support Agreement before paying.

## Home Support timing notice

> The APC Home Support Programme contains four 60-minute online sessions, normally delivered about once every two weeks over approximately six to eight weeks. The parties should aim to complete it within 12 weeks after the first paid session. The 12-week point is a review point, not automatic expiry. A written pause or extension may be agreed for a material interruption. There is no additional post-programme check-in or unlimited messaging.

> Recommended payment structure: RM1,800 total, collected as four RM450 milestone payments. A milestone is charged only when that stage is ready to begin. Once a milestone begins, its payment is not refunded for parent change of mind, cancellation, inactivity, or non-attendance. Future milestones that have not begun are not charged.

## Home Support WhatsApp notice

> During an active Home Support Programme, one designated parent may send one consolidated plan-implementation update or question per calendar week by WhatsApp, as up to five short bullet points or one voice note up to three minutes. APC normally gives one substantive reply within two business days, Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Malaysia time, excluding federal and Selangor public holidays and announced closures, plus one brief clarification if needed. New concerns, lengthy histories, file or observation reviews, and urgent or emergency matters are outside this channel.

3. Record the parent identity, service, policy and agreement versions, date, time, and affirmative acceptance.

5. Do not configure an automatic no-show forfeiture, automatic refund denial, or automatic programme closure.
6. Keep event descriptions consistent with the website and agreement.
7. Display the complete price and current no-additional-fee/tax wording before payment; do not infer tax exemption from sole-proprietor status.
8. Provide the mandatory online disclosures in Bahasa Malaysia, with English as an additional version if desired.
