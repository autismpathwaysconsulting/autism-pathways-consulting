## Phase 4 — Every enquiry: qualify before selling

For every institutional lead, record:
- organisation/location,
- decision-maker role,
- age group,
- team size,
- main problem,
- desired practice change,
- requested format,
- timeline,
- recognition/HRD requirements,
- budget signal,
- objection,
- next step.

Then route:
- early language/interaction → consider Teacher Talk,
- deeper whole-setting early-language practice change → consider LLLI,
- emergent literacy ages 3–5 → consider ABC and Beyond,
- autism-specific implementation → APC consultation/PD,
- regulated clinical request → decline/refer.

## Phase 6 — Pricing

Current internal Teacher Talk A hypothesis is **organisation/cohort pricing**, not per-person pricing.

Working professional-fee range:
- roughly RM4,800–RM6,000 total for a smaller institutional cohort around up to 12 educators,
- PLUS required participant materials,
- PLUS travel/venue/accommodation where applicable,
- follow-up implementation support either included by design or separately itemised.

This is not RM4,800–RM6,000 × 12.

At 12 participants, the professional fee works out to roughly RM400–RM500 per educator before materials/travel, but the client pays the organisation-level quote.

### Online versus in-person
Start with the **same core professional-fee logic** because the live teaching hours and professional responsibility remain similar.

In-person may add:
- travel,
- parking/tolls,
- accommodation/interstate costs,
- venue/AV if APC supplies them.

Online may reduce those logistics but should not automatically be deeply discounted.

Do not publish this pricing until 5–10 qualified buyer conversations test it.

## Phase 7 — First serious meeting

Use `EDUCATOR_PD_MEETING_DEMO.md`.

Meeting sequence:
1. ask for one recurring staff problem,
2. show evidence for implementation/coaching,
3. explain APC's `identify → learn → practise → implement → review` model,
4. explain Hanen lane versus APC autism-specific lane,
5. run the synthetic prompting/independence demo,
6. ask procurement/HRD/PD-recognition questions,
7. offer to send a written recommendation rather than forcing a course.

Do not show proprietary Hanen slides/videos in the sales demo.

## Phase 8 — First paid pilot

Preferred first pilot:
- one institutional Teacher Talk A cohort after readiness clears,
- or one APC autism-specific focused PD pilot if demand is stronger there.

For the pilot, collect:
- pre-training need/confidence,
- attendance,
- action plan,
- immediate feedback,
- 3–4 week implementation follow-up,
- leadership summary.

Do not use testimonials/outcomes publicly without consent.

## Phase 9 — After 3–5 institutional conversations

Review evidence:
- which problems repeat,
- who buys,
- whether Hanen recognition matters,
- whether HRD claimability blocks purchase,
- preferred cohort size,
- online/in-person demand,
- pricing resistance,
- desired follow-up.

Only then decide whether HRD Corp is worth pursuing.

## Phase 10 — HRD Corp decision

Pursue HRD TTT / accreditation if:
- multiple qualified buyers say claimability materially affects purchase,
- APC wants employer-funded Malaysian corporate/education training at scale.

Do not pursue it merely to make APC PD “official”.

## Phase 11 — International expansion

Sequence:
1. prove Malaysia offer,
2. verify Hanen cross-border permission,
3. interview Singapore buyers,
4. set SGD institutional pricing based on local market and buyer requirements,
5. check local scope/privacy/tax requirements,
6. pilot one cross-border educator engagement,
7. only then widen reach.

Cross-border educator PD is strategically cleaner than cross-border parent support because parent/clinical scope can vary by country.

## Phase 12 — Stronger recognition later

For APC-owned courses only, after pilots:
- independent international CPD accreditation,
- HRD Corp claimability if useful,
- MQA stand-alone microcredential pathway for a substantial assessed course,
- university partnership,
- IACET much later if international continuing-education infrastructure justifies the cost.

Do not place Hanen-owned curriculum inside an APC-owned accredited microcredential without written Hanen permission.

## Phase 13 — Website/content integration

Keep three clear business lanes:
- Parents,

- Products / tools.

Content should expose problems before selling courses:
- implementation inconsistency,
- knowing theory but struggling in the moment,
- participation versus compliance,

Use educational content to route leaders to `/schools` rather than turning every social post into course promotion.

## Hard stop rules

Stop and verify before proceeding if:
- a buyer asks for clinical speech/language work,
- an organisation wants CJ to certify its staff as Hanen/SCERTS providers,
- buyer asks for an unapproved Hanen adaptation,
- buyer wants recordings reused outside the programme purpose,
- cross-border permission is unclear,
- HRD/KPM/CPD recognition wording is unclear,
- CJ cannot answer a programme question confidently,
- privacy/consent for video feedback is incomplete.

## Weekend success definition

Success is NOT:
- likes,
- Story votes,
- compliments.

Success signals are:
- a principal/director/SENCO/centre owner enquires,
- shares a real organisational problem,
- agrees to a needs call,
- asks for programme structure/quote,
- gives cohort size/timeline,
- or asks procurement questions.

Those signals determine whether APC should invest further.
