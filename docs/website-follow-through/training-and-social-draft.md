## Draft one-page offer

### From Concern to Conversation
**Practical next steps for educators · 90-minute facilitated workshop**

You have noticed something that concerns you. How do you describe it fairly, begin a conversation with the family and agree what support can happen next?

This workshop gives school and early-years teams time to practise those steps using fictional classroom examples. We work through what staff have observed, what remains uncertain and how to invite a parent's perspective without assigning a diagnosis.

**What participants practise**

1. Write a specific observation, including the situation and support provided.
2. Separate that observation from a possible explanation.
3. Prepare a respectful opening for a private parent conversation.
4. Explain that screening helps identify the need for further evaluation and does not establish a diagnosis.
5. Choose a practical classroom adjustment, agree who will follow up and decide what to review.

**Proposed take-home tools:** an observation sheet, a conversation planner and a next-step checklist. Drafts are below; final teaching materials and rehearsal remain outstanding.

**Enquire:** Share your organisation, children's age range, team size, main concern, preferred format and possible dates. Please leave out names, photographs and identifiable student information.

Scope, format, fees and booking terms are confirmed in writing after a suitability discussion. Enquiring does not reserve a date.

## Three original APC worksheet drafts

### 1. Observation before interpretation

Use a fictional case during training. Follow the school's own recording and privacy process in practice.

| Field | Prompt / writing space |
| --- | --- |
| Situation | What activity was taking place? What was expected? |
| Observation | What did the child do or say? Record observable detail. |
| Adult/environment support | What instruction, demonstration, communication option or adjustment was available? |
| What followed | What happened after the support or response? |
| Child's perspective | What have they communicated? If unknown, write unknown. |
| Possible explanation | What might contribute? Label this as a possibility, not a finding. |
| Other possibilities | What else could explain the same observation? |
| Question to clarify | What information or perspective is missing? |

**Fictional example:** “During group instructions, the student waited. After the teacher showed the first action, the student began.” This does not establish autism or explain the student's internal experience. A question to explore is whether the original instruction was accessible.

### 2. Parent-conversation planner

- Purpose of the conversation: ____________________
- Specific observation to share: ____________________
- A genuine strength or interest to include, if known: ____________________
- Opening: “We have noticed [observation] during [situation]. What have you noticed?”
- What the family wants staff to understand: ____________________
- What we do not yet know: ____________________
- Support already tried and what followed: ____________________
- Agreed next step, without pressure to accept a label: ____________________
- School lead and follow-up date: ____________________

Use a private, appropriate school communication channel. Do not turn a training role-play into assessment of a real child. If there is an urgent welfare concern, follow the school's safeguarding procedures rather than waiting for a workshop follow-up.

### 3. Support and review checklist

- [ ] The goal describes access, participation or communication, not simply compliance.
- [ ] The child and family's perspectives have been invited in an accessible way.
- [ ] The proposed adjustment fits the identified situation and staff role.
- [ ] Necessary existing support is maintained.
- [ ] A named adult knows what to try and what to record.
- [ ] The team has agreed when to review and how to respond if distress increases.
- [ ] Any referral discussion follows the school's process and appropriate professional advice.
- [ ] No diagnosis or outcome guarantee is implied.
