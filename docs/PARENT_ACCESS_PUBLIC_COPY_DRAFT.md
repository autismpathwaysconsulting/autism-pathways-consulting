# APC Parent Access — Public Copy Draft

Status: Draft only. Do not publish until CJ approves whether supported-rate places and instalments will be offered.

## Website FAQ option

### What if the standard fee is not affordable for my family?

APC keeps the Free 15-Min First Step Call available so families can still clarify the most appropriate next route before paying for support.

If paid APC support is a good fit but the standard fee would prevent access, a small number of supported-rate places may be available for Malaysian families. Availability is limited because CJ must keep the service sustainable and maintain enough time for each active family.

For the Home Implementation Programme, an instalment option may also be available.

If paid APC support is not workable, CJ can still point you toward relevant free or lower-cost resources where appropriate.

## Shorter services-page wording

> **Cost is a barrier?** A small number of supported-rate places may be available for Malaysian families when the standard fee would otherwise prevent access. Ask during the First Step Call. Availability is limited.

## First Step Call wording when parent raises cost

> "If APC is a good fit but the standard fee is not workable for your family right now, tell me. I may have a limited supported-rate place available, and if not I can still point you toward the most relevant free or lower-cost route I know."

Do not promise a supported place before checking capacity.

## Supported-rate internal explanation if asked

> "The supported rate is the same service scope at a reduced fee. It is not a shorter or lower-quality version. I keep the number of supported places limited so I can sustain the time required for each family."

## Instalment wording

> "The Home Implementation Programme can be split into two instalments if that makes the programme easier to manage financially. The total fee and programme scope remain the same."

## Free-resource route

If paid support is unsuitable/unavailable, APC can point parents to:
- APC Calm,
- relevant APC public resources,
- reputable public resources,
- WHO Caregiver Skills Training online materials where appropriate.

Do not present the WHO programme as APC-delivered or as a substitute for individual professional care when a family needs regulated assessment/treatment.

## What not to say

Avoid:
- "low-income discount"
- "hardship proof required"
- "charity slot"
- "financial aid" unless a formal aid programme exists
- "everyone deserves this so I will always make it work"
- promises of unlimited reduced-fee availability

Prefer:
- supported-rate place
- access place
- limited supported fee
- instalment option

## Transparency rule

If supported-rate places are publicly mentioned, APC should also clearly state:
- availability is limited,
- same service quality/scope,
- no guarantee of a place,
- eligibility is based on the standard fee being a genuine barrier,
- no unnecessary financial documentation is required.
