# Teacher Talk™ A - CJ Delivery Readiness Rubric

Status: Structural rubric only. Content-specific scoring criteria will be populated from the current licensed Teacher Talk A materials after upload.

## Readiness rule

Target overall score: **>= 8.5 / 10**

A critical fidelity/licensing/scope error overrides the numerical score and results in **NOT READY**.

## 1. Programme purpose and audience - 10%

CJ can accurately explain:
- who the training is for,
- what the training is designed to help educators do,
- what it is not,
- prerequisites / sequence rules,
- how it differs from APC autism-specific PD.

Score 0-10.

## 2. Learning objectives / key concepts - 20%

CJ can explain the programme's required concepts accurately and in plain language without changing their meaning.

Check:
- factual accuracy,
- no omitted critical teaching point,
- no invented Hanen claim,
- clear distinction between programme content and CJ's additional examples/opinions.

Score 0-10.

## 3. Session structure and timing - 15%

CJ can:
- follow the authorised session structure,
- keep required activities/media within the intended sequence,
- finish within the programme time,
- preserve time for participant practice/reflection.

Score 0-10.

## 4. Facilitation quality - 15%

CJ:
- does not read slides word-for-word,
- gives clear instructions,
- facilitates discussion without drifting off programme purpose,
- uses participant examples appropriately,
- keeps the tone practical and respectful,
- handles silence / dominant participants / tangents.

Score 0-10.

## 5. Participant application - 10%

CJ can help participants connect learning to their daily educational setting without:
- turning the session into case-specific therapy,
- giving diagnosis,
- overclaiming child outcomes,
- altering the official programme.

Score 0-10.

## 6. Questions and difficult scenarios - 15%

CJ can accurately answer common programme questions and safely defer questions when unsure.

Include red-team prompts such as:
- “Does this make me Hanen certified?”
- “Can I teach this to my colleagues?”
- “Can you diagnose this child's communication problem?”
- “Can we skip this activity?”
- “Can you give us the slides?”
- “Can I record the training?”
- “Is this autism therapy?”
- “Can you adapt this for parents?”

Content-specific questions to be added after current materials are uploaded.

Score 0-10.

## 7. Licence / copyright / scope safety - 10%

CJ can state and follow:
- what materials may be shown / distributed,
- recording restrictions,
- programme branding rules,
- participant-documentation limits,
- professional-scope boundaries,
- no SLT/SLP misrepresentation.

Score 0-10.

## 8. Operational delivery - 5%

Ready:
- workbook/material count,
- attendance process,
- participant communication,
- online / in-person tech,
- break timing,
- room setup,
- backup plan for technical failure,
- evaluation / completion record.

Score 0-10.

## Critical fail conditions

Any of the following = NOT READY regardless of overall score:
- inaccurate core programme teaching,
- inability to distinguish Teacher Talk from autism-specific PD,
- omission of a mandatory component,
- unauthorised adaptation,
- unauthorised distribution/recording of protected materials,
- telling participants they become licensed/certified Hanen providers,
- clinical speech-language claim outside APC scope,
- unresolved uncertainty about required materials / participant eligibility,
- unable to complete the required content within time.

## Rehearsal sequence

### Pass 1 - knowledge
Closed-book oral/written questions.

### Pass 2 - section rehearsal
Deliver selected difficult blocks and activities.

### Pass 3 - Q&A red team
Rapid questions, scope traps and participant objections.

### Pass 4 - full timed rehearsal
Run the training in sequence with realistic transitions.

### Pass 5 - final review
Fix errors, retest only weak sections, then re-run critical Q&A.
