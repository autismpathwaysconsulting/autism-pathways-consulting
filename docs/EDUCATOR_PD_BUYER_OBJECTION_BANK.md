# APC Educator PD — Buyer Objection & Response Bank

Status: Internal sales-preparation guide. Use only claims that are currently verified.

## 1. "Why should we pay APC when our teachers can watch free autism webinars?"

Response:
Free information can be useful. APC's paid work is for teams that need a structured learning process, application to their setting, implementation planning and review. The value is not simply access to information.

Do not disparage free resources.

## 2. "Is this autism certification?"

Response:
No. APC should distinguish between:
- licensed Hanen educator programmes CJ is authorised to provide,
- APC autism-specific professional development/consultation,
- professional-development completion documentation.

Attendance does not automatically make participants certified autism practitioners or Hanen providers.

## 3. "Is it HRD Corp claimable?"

Current safe response:
Not currently advertised as HRD Corp claimable. APC is evaluating whether that route is commercially useful based on buyer demand.

Record every time this question affects purchase intent.

## 4. "Is it KPM recognised?"

Response:
APC does not currently claim KPM accreditation or recognised CPD points. A school may still choose to purchase professional development for its own internal development needs, but formal recognition should not be implied without written confirmation.

## 5. "Will this improve our students' autism outcomes?"

Response:
APC does not guarantee child outcomes. For professional development, the immediate measurable targets are educator learning, implementation and changes in practice. Where appropriate, the organisation can agree on how implementation will be reviewed.

## 6. "Why Hanen?"

Use only after the buyer's problem actually matches the programme.

Safe framing:
Hanen provides structured educator programmes with defined learning objectives and official programme materials. CJ holds the relevant current certification/licence to deliver specific programmes. The fit depends on the team's actual need, age group and setting.

Do not pitch Hanen as generic autism training.

## 7. "Why not just give us a 2-hour version?"

For official Hanen programmes:
The programme must be delivered within the formats permitted by Hanen. APC does not remove mandatory elements simply to fit a buyer's preferred duration.

For APC-owned training:
A shorter focused session may be possible when the objective is narrower, but it should not be presented as equivalent to a full licensed programme.

## 8. "Can we record the training for absent staff?"

Default response for licensed programmes:
No recording should be promised. Programme-owner restrictions and participant privacy apply. Confirm exact rules against current licensed materials before each programme.

For APC-owned sessions, recording is only considered under separately agreed terms and privacy/IP conditions.

## 9. "Can we reuse your slides for internal training?"

Licensed Hanen material:
No. Do not authorise redistribution of trainer-only/proprietary materials unless the programme owner explicitly permits it.

APC-owned material:
Participant use within the agreed organisation may be allowed under the stated terms, but reproduction/resale/training others from the materials requires written permission.

## 10. "Can our SENCO deliver this to everyone else after attending?"

Response:
Attending professional development does not automatically grant trainer rights. Participants may apply what they learn in their own practice, but cannot represent themselves as licensed trainers/providers unless the relevant programme owner explicitly grants that status.

## 11. "Can you customise the Hanen course to our school?"

Response:
CJ can contextualise discussion and implementation where the programme allows, but cannot alter required programme content/format beyond what Hanen permits. APC should verify current adaptation rules before agreeing to customisation.

## 12. "Do you work with secondary/teen students?"

For Hanen early-childhood programmes:
These specific Hanen educator programmes are designed for early childhood contexts and should not be sold as teen programmes.

For APC autism-specific consultation:
CJ may have relevant school/teen experience, but the scope should be defined separately and accurately.

## 13. "Are you a therapist?"

Response:
No. APC is an education, consultation and professional-development service. CJ does not provide speech-language therapy, diagnosis or other regulated clinical services through APC.

## 14. "Can you assess our students?"

Response:
APC can support educational observation/consultation within scope where appropriately contracted, but does not provide diagnostic/clinical assessment. If the request is for formal clinical assessment, refer to the appropriate registered professional.

## 15. "Can you train us on SCERTS?"

Response:
CJ has completed SCERTS professional-development training and may draw on that professional learning in his educational work. APC does not currently advertise an official SCERTS certification/trainer course.

Do not reproduce proprietary SCERTS training materials without permission.

## 16. "Can you do the course in Singapore/Australia?"

Response:
Cross-border delivery is reviewed case by case. Confirm programme-owner territorial permission, local regulatory/scope requirements, privacy/data rules, tax and delivery logistics before accepting.

## 17. "Why is your institutional fee higher than an individual webinar?"

Response:
Institutional delivery includes preparation, defined learning objectives, live facilitation, participant materials/administration, implementation planning and, where included, follow-up. It is not priced as access to a recording.

## 18. "Can we pay per participant instead of per organisation?"

Response:
APC can quote either model if commercially sensible, but institutional pricing should reflect the fixed preparation/delivery work plus participant-dependent materials/admin. Do not automatically reduce the total below sustainable delivery cost.

## 19. "Can parents attend Teacher Talk/LLLI/ABC with our staff?"

Do not answer from memory. Confirm the current programme audience/participant requirements from the current Leader Guide before accepting mixed audiences.

## 20. "Can you issue a certificate?"

For APC-owned training:
A truthful APC Certificate of Completion can document title, date, hours and completion criteria.

For Hanen programmes:
Participant documentation must follow Hanen's current requirements. Do not promise Hanen-branded certificates or provider status unless verified.

## 21. "Why not ABA training?"

Response:
APC should not frame the offer as anti-ABA or compete by attacking another approach. Explain what APC actually provides: inclusive-education perspective, licensed Hanen programmes where appropriate, practical implementation support, and autism-specific educational consultation.

## 22. "Can you guarantee staff will implement it?"

Response:
No. APC can design for implementation through practice, action planning, feedback and follow-up, but organisational conditions still matter. Leadership support, time, consistency and staff capacity affect implementation.

## 23. "What if staff disagree with the approach?"

Response:
Professional development should allow questions and critical discussion. CJ should distinguish evidence, programme requirements and professional judgement, and should not pressure participants to claim agreement they do not hold. The goal is accurate understanding and responsible application.

## 24. "Can we use a real student case during the training?"

Response:
Prefer de-identified examples. If identifiable information is genuinely necessary, privacy/consent and safeguarding requirements must be established in advance. Do not invite casual disclosure of student-sensitive information.

## 25. "Can you send us the research?"

Response:
Yes. APC can provide a short evidence summary using public sources and programme-owner research information, clearly separating what the research shows from what it does not prove.

Do not send proprietary member-only training content as evidence handouts.
