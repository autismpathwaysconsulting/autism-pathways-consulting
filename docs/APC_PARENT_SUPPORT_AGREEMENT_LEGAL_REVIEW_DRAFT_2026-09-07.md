## 2. APC's role

APC provides parent education, pattern review, and structured parent support. It is not therapy, diagnosis, clinical treatment, medical advice, emergency support, or a substitute for the professionals already supporting the child. APC does not guarantee a particular result.

## 3. Purchased scope

### One-Concern Parent Session, RM350

One 45-minute online session for one repeated concern. It includes focused pattern review, one practical next step, and a concise written next-step summary. It does not include ongoing adjustment, continuing message support, or a follow-up session.

### APC Home Support Programme, RM1,800

Four 60-minute online sessions, normally delivered about once every two weeks over approximately six to eight weeks. It includes a personalised Home Support Plan, implementation review and adjustment, bounded weekly parent updates and brief WhatsApp clarification about the agreed plan during the active programme, and a final written plan or summary. It does not include an additional post-programme check-in, crisis support, unrelated case work, or unlimited messaging.

**Recommended payment structure:** RM1,800 total, collected as four RM450 milestone payments. APC collects each milestone payment only when the parties are ready to begin that milestone. This prevents APC from holding payment for future work that has not started and is the recommended structure for APC's no-change-of-mind-refund policy.

Only the selected service applies.

### Price disclosure

The stated price is the total amount APC currently charges for the selected service. APC does not currently add a separate booking fee or service-tax amount. This describes APC's present charging practice; it is not a representation that sole proprietors are exempt from tax. If APC becomes legally required to collect a tax or other mandatory charge, the complete revised total must be disclosed and accepted before payment. No undisclosed amount may be added after acceptance.

## 4. Scheduling and completion

The parties should arrange provisional Home Support dates at onboarding and should aim to complete the programme within 12 weeks after the first paid session. The 12-week point is a review point, not automatic expiry. A pause or extension may be agreed in writing for illness, family circumstances, safety needs, APC availability, or another material interruption. The written record should state a restart date or review date.

If a programme remains unfinished, APC will confirm the recorded sessions delivered and remaining and ask the parent to book, agree a dated pause, or discuss early closure. APC will not automatically forfeit a paid session or decide a refund solely because the intended period has passed.

## 5. Rescheduling, missed sessions, and late arrival

- With at least 24 hours' notice, a paid appointment may be rescheduled without losing the session, subject to availability.
- With less than 24 hours' notice, or for a no-show, the appointment normally counts as used, subject to exceptional-circumstances review, this agreement, and applicable legal rights.
- Late arrival does not normally extend the scheduled end time.
- If APC cancels, the parent does not lose the session and APC will offer a replacement date.
- No system makes an automatic forfeiture, refund, credit, or consumer-rights decision.

## 6. Cancellations, early closure, credits, and legally required remedies

The following prospective rule applies only when it was supplied and accepted before payment. **APC does not provide refunds because a parent changes their mind, changes priorities, becomes unavailable, or decides not to continue.** This rule does not remove a remedy required by Malaysian law and does not allow APC to retain payment for work APC refuses or becomes unable to deliver.

- One-Concern Parent Session: payment secures the appointment and APC's preparation. With at least 24 hours' notice, the parent may use one reschedule, subject to availability. APC does not provide a cash refund for parent cancellation or non-attendance. With less than 24 hours' notice, or for a no-show, the session normally counts as used.
- Home Support Programme: the recommended structure is four RM450 milestone payments. A milestone begins only when APC sends a written milestone-start confirmation, no earlier than 48 hours before its scheduled session. Once a milestone begins, its payment is non-refundable for parent change of mind, cancellation, non-attendance, inactivity, or early closure. A future milestone that has not begun must not be charged. With at least 24 hours' notice, the parent may reschedule the session attached to an active milestone, subject to availability.
- Full advance payment: APC should not collect the entire RM1,800 in advance while operating a no-change-of-mind-refund policy. If APC later chooses to offer full advance payment, the agreement must separately state the lawful treatment of unperformed future work and pass Malaysian legal review before use.
- APC cancellation or inability to deliver: APC will first offer a reasonably suitable replacement. If APC cannot deliver the paid service or milestone within 14 calendar days of the original date, the parent may choose another agreed date or the refund or other remedy required for the undelivered work. This is not a change-of-mind refund.
- Service failure or mandatory rights: where Malaysian law gives the parent a right to correction, repeat performance, cancellation, refund, compensation, or another remedy, this agreement does not remove it.
- Credits: for a parent-requested exception, APC may offer a dated service credit or additional reschedule. A credit does not replace a mandatory legal remedy unless the parent lawfully agrees after the issue arises.

An exceptional-circumstances review is available for sudden illness, hospitalisation, bereavement, serious family or safety disruption, material technical failure outside the parent's reasonable control, or another comparably serious event. The parent should contact APC as soon as reasonably possible. APC records the facts considered and may offer a service credit or additional reschedule. APC applies the same criteria consistently and may request proportionate supporting information without requiring unnecessary sensitive details.

APC, not an automated system, makes the operational decision and gives reasons in writing. Nothing in this section excludes or limits any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

## 7. Parent responsibilities and safety

The parent decides whether a suggested step is safe and practical in the family's circumstances and remains responsible for supervision, medical decisions, and obtaining emergency or qualified professional help when needed. The parent agrees to give information that is reasonably accurate and relevant and to tell APC when circumstances materially change.

## 8. Communication boundaries

WhatsApp clarification is included only with the Home Support Programme and only during an active programme or dated written extension. It begins after Session 1 and ends when the final written plan or summary is supplied, unless the parties record a different end date.

- Channel: the designated APC WhatsApp number and one designated parent contact.
- Availability: Monday to Friday, 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Malaysia time, excluding federal and Selangor public holidays and announced closure periods.
- Parent allowance: one consolidated update or question each calendar week, as text of up to five short bullet points or one voice note of up to three minutes.
- APC response: one substantive reply within two business days, plus one brief clarification if needed. A reply may be delayed during a notified closure, illness, or emergency; APC will give a revised response time when reasonably possible.
- Scope: implementation questions about the current agreed plan and session logistics. A new concern, lengthy history, document review, observation request, or issue requiring assessment is moved to the next scheduled session or a separately agreed service.
- Media: do not send recordings of a child, school records, medical reports, or other highly sensitive files through WhatsApp unless APC has specifically requested the minimum necessary item and the approved privacy process permits it.

WhatsApp is not continuously monitored and is not urgent, crisis, clinical, safeguarding, or emergency support. In an emergency, the parent should contact the appropriate Malaysian emergency or qualified local service. Between-session support pauses while the programme is dormant and resumes only when a dated restart is recorded.

## 9. Personal information and child information

The parent confirms that they are authorised to share information about the child and any other person. The parent agrees to share only information reasonably needed for the service. Child health or diagnosis information may be sensitive personal data. APC must provide the approved Privacy Policy and record any express consent required before processing.

Identifiable client information must not be stored in Cloudflare D1 or entered into a generative AI service until APC has completed the relevant privacy, security, processor, cross-border transfer, retention, and notice or consent controls.

Unless a longer period is legally required or a dispute requires a documented hold, APC's proposed retention limits are: enquiry records for 12 months after the last meaningful contact; detailed intake, facilitator, session, and child-related working notes for 24 months after service completion or closure; essential agreement, acceptance, invoice, payment, refund, and accounting records for seven years from the applicable tax-return year; material complaints and dispute records for seven years after closure; operational WhatsApp content for 90 days after service completion once material service decisions have been transferred to the controlled record; security and access logs for 12 months; and recoverable backups for no more than 90 further days. APC should delete or irreversibly anonymise records when the applicable period ends.

## 10. Materials
