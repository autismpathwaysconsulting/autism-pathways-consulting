## 5. What to observe

Keep observation simple. Notice:
- how often the situation occurs,
- what tends to happen immediately before it,
- whether the agreed adult response was used,
- what changed afterwards,
- any unexpected effects.

Do not turn home life into constant data collection.

## 6. How we will know this needs review

Review sooner if:
- the concern becomes significantly more frequent/intense,
- there is a new safety concern,
- the child appears distressed or unwell,
- the agreed change consistently makes the situation worse,
- new information suggests the original hypothesis was wrong.
