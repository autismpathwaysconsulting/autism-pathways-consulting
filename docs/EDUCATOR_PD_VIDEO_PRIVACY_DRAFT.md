# APC Educator PD — Video Feedback Privacy & Consent Draft

Status: Internal draft. Required before paid LLLI / ABC video-feedback delivery. This is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## 1. Purpose

Some licensed Hanen educator programmes require individual video feedback involving an educator interacting with one or more children in the classroom. APC must not begin collecting or receiving such recordings until a clear consent, security, retention and deletion process is in place.

## 2. Minimum principle

Collect the minimum information and footage needed for the professional-development purpose.

Do not reuse training footage for:
- social media,
- marketing,
- testimonials,
- APC-owned courses,
- research/publication,
- AI model training,
- unrelated client work.

Any separate future use would require its own explicit legal/ethical basis and permissions.

## 3. Required permissions before recording

Before any child appears in a recording, APC should require the host organisation to confirm that it has obtained all permissions required by its policies and applicable law.

APC should also use a written consent/acknowledgement process covering at minimum:
- purpose of recording,
- who may view it,
- how it will be transferred,
- where it will be stored,
- how long it will be retained,
- when/how it will be deleted,
- that participation in marketing/publication is not part of the purpose,
- contact point for privacy questions.

Where parent/guardian consent is required, the organisation should obtain it before recording.

## 4. De-identification where practical

Prefer:
- no child full names in filenames,
- no DOB, medical ID or school ID in filenames,
- neutral participant codes,
- limiting camera framing to what is necessary,
- avoiding unrelated children where practical,
- avoiding visible confidential documents/screens.

## 5. Transfer

Do not accept classroom video through ordinary social-media DMs.

Preferred transfer method should be a controlled, access-restricted channel agreed in advance.

If the organisation provides the recording:
- specify who uploads it,
- specify the exact destination,
- restrict access to CJ / authorised personnel only,
- do not create unnecessary duplicate copies.

## 6. Storage

Minimum controls:
- password-protected account/device,
- device encryption enabled,
- strong unique passwords,
- MFA where available,
- no shared/public links,
- no unrestricted cloud folders,
- access log or file register where practical.

## 7. Retention

Default principle: retain only as long as needed to complete the programme feedback and any agreed quality/documentation period.

Before launch, APC must choose and document a specific retention period rather than using “indefinitely”.

The period must also be compatible with Hanen programme requirements and the host organisation's policy.

## 8. Deletion

At the end of the approved retention period:
- delete the working copy,
- delete known duplicates/backups where technically possible,
- record deletion date in the file register,
- notify the organisation if the agreement requires confirmation.

## 9. Live video feedback

Where live observation/coaching can meet programme requirements without retaining a recording, prefer the least-data approach compatible with the Hanen Leader Guide and programme requirements.

Do not assume live-only delivery is permitted unless the relevant Hanen programme instructions allow it.

## 10. Breach / wrong-file procedure

If APC receives the wrong file or a file containing unexpected sensitive/identifying information:
1. stop further use,
2. restrict access,
3. document what was received,
4. inform the organisation promptly,
5. agree secure deletion/next steps,
6. assess whether any legal breach-notification duties apply.

## 11. Organisation responsibilities to put in the agreement

The host organisation should be responsible for:
- confirming it has authority to organise the recording,
- obtaining required parent/guardian/staff permissions,
- following its own safeguarding and privacy policies,
- providing accurate consent status,
- preventing unauthorised recording during the session.

APC remains responsible for its own handling of any recording once received/accessed.

## 12. APC register fields

For every video file accessed/received:
- internal file code,
- programme,
- educator participant,
- organisation,
- date recorded,
- consent confirmation received date,
- date received/accessed,
- storage location,
- permitted viewers,
- retention deadline,
- deletion date,
- notes/incidents.

Do not put unnecessary child identity information in the register.

## 14. Public-facing wording

Do not publish detailed storage mechanics unless appropriate. A concise buyer-facing explanation may be:

“Where an official programme requires individual video feedback, recording is only used for the agreed professional-development purpose and is handled under a separate consent and privacy process. APC does not use classroom training recordings for social media or marketing.”
