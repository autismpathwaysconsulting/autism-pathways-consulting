# APC School & Educator PD — Response Templates

These are internal templates. Adapt details to the actual enquiry. Do not promise availability, programme fit, certification, HRD claimability or cross-border delivery before checking.

## 1. Initial WhatsApp reply — default

Hi [Name], thank you for reaching out and for sharing a little about [organisation].

Before I recommend a programme, I would like to understand what your team is trying to handle more confidently or consistently in practice.

Could you send me:

1. the age group your team supports,
2. approximately how many educators would be involved,
3. one or two recurring situations you would most like the team to handle better,
4. whether you are looking for in-person or online support, and
5. whether your organisation has any specific professional-development or HRD Corp requirements?

Please keep the examples de-identified and avoid sharing student names or other identifying information here.

Once I understand the need, I can tell you whether one of the structured educator programmes or a more focused APC consultation would be the better fit.

CJ
Autism Pathways Consulting

## 2. Leadership call invitation

Thank you. Based on what you have described, I think it would be more useful to have a short leadership/needs conversation before deciding on a programme.

The purpose would be to clarify:

- the main practice gap,
- who needs to be involved,
- what you would want staff doing differently afterwards,
- the most appropriate training format, and
- whether follow-up implementation support is needed.

I do not want to recommend a course simply because it is available if it is not the right fit for your team.

If you are happy to proceed, I can send you some possible times.

## 3. Teacher Talk may fit

Thank you for the additional context.

From what you have described, Teacher Talk™ may be worth discussing because the need appears to be around early-childhood educator interaction and supporting language/social development during everyday routines.

Teacher Talk is a structured Hanen early-childhood professional-development series. It is not an autism-specific programme. Training A is the foundation of the series and is 7.5 hours.

Before I recommend it formally, I would still want to confirm the participant group, delivery format, materials and scheduling requirements with you.

## 4. ABC and Beyond may fit

Thank you for the additional context.

The need you described appears closely related to emergent literacy / shared reading practice, so ABC and Beyond™ may be one option to discuss.

ABC and Beyond is a structured Hanen programme for early childhood educators working with children around ages 3–5. Depending on the need, the official programme may be delivered in its full format or through specific Hanen-authorised modules.

I would first confirm which authorised format actually matches your goals rather than shortening or modifying the programme informally.

## 5. APC autism-specific support may fit

Thank you for clarifying. This sounds more like an autism-specific implementation need than a Hanen early-childhood language/literacy programme need.

A focused APC educator consultation or professional-development session may be more appropriate, particularly around [inclusion / regulation / transitions / prompting and independence / team consistency / parent-school collaboration].

This would be APC's own educational/consultative work, separate from the Hanen curriculum.

The next useful step would be to define the specific practice change your team wants and the staff who need to be involved.

## 6. HRD Corp question

APC is not currently advertising these programmes as HRD Corp claimable.

I am tracking whether HRD Corp claimability is an important procurement requirement for the organisations approaching APC. If it is essential for your organisation, please let me know, because that affects whether the current offer is suitable for you.

## 7. Certificate question

Participants are completing professional development, not becoming licensed/certified Hanen providers or trainers through attendance.

For official Hanen programmes, participant documentation will follow the requirements of the relevant Hanen programme. I am also confirming the exact participant-certificate wording and format with Hanen before publishing any certificate claim.

For APC-owned professional development, APC can provide a truthful Certificate of Completion documenting the training title, date, hours and completion criteria. It does not confer professional licensure.

## 8. Outcome guarantee question

I do not guarantee child outcomes from professional development.

The outcomes I can responsibly focus on are what educators learn, what they implement, what barriers appear in practice and what needs to be adjusted. If useful, we can agree on an implementation follow-up so the training does not end at attendance alone.

## 9. Cross-border enquiry

Thank you for asking. I can review an international educator-training request, but I do not automatically accept cross-border delivery for every programme.

The answer depends on the specific programme licence, format, participant location and local requirements. I will verify those points before confirming that I can provide the training in [country].

## 10. Decline / out of scope — clinical request

Thank you for explaining what you are looking for.

That request falls outside APC's scope because it involves [speech-language assessment / therapy / diagnosis / psychology / medical or crisis support]. APC provides education, consultation and professional development rather than regulated clinical services.

I do not want to blur that boundary. A suitably registered professional in the relevant discipline would be the more appropriate route for that part of the request.

## 11. Not ready to quote yet

Thank you. I have enough information to understand the broad need, but not enough to price it responsibly yet.

The fee depends on the programme/format, number of participants, required materials, delivery schedule, location and whether implementation follow-up is included.

I would prefer to send you a clear scope and quote after the needs conversation rather than give you a number that changes later.
