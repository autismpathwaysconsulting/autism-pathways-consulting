## Approximately 30-day maintenance check

Questions:
- Is the plan still being used?
- Has it become easier/harder to sustain?
- Is the original concern still the same?
- Has a new barrier appeared?
- Does the family need another APC implementation cycle, a single progress review, or another professional route?

### Maintenance decision

- Continue independently
- Minor adjustment only
- Focused APC Progress Review may help
- Another APC implementation cycle may help
- Refer / coordinate with another professional

## Messaging boundary

During the active programme, WhatsApp clarification is for questions about the agreed plan and observations.

It does not include:
- unlimited on-demand consultation,
- a new unrelated concern,
- emergency/crisis support,
- diagnosis or clinical treatment.

If the concern changes substantially, CJ may recommend scheduling another session or using another professional service.
