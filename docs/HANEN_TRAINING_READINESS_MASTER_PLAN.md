# APC Hanen Training Readiness Master Plan

Status: Internal readiness architecture. Proprietary programme-content review begins only after CJ provides the current licensed materials.

## 1. Objective

Move from “CJ holds the licence/certification” to “CJ is current, fluent, operationally ready and able to deliver the programme with high fidelity and clear scope.”

The first paid public course date should not be advertised until the programme-specific readiness gate is passed.

## 2. Verified APC Hanen portfolio

Current business planning recognises:
- Learning Language and Loving It™: CJ is a certified member/licensed programme leader while membership is current.
- Teacher Talk™ Training Series: delivery licence included through CJ's LLLI certification.
- ABC and Beyond™: CJ completed certification and holds programme delivery rights subject to current membership/licence requirements.

SPARK Communication™ is intentionally excluded from the GREEN-only APC commercial plan because CJ does not want Malaysian speech-language scope ambiguity.

Hanen membership renewal must remain current. Current known renewal date: 24 October 2026.

## 3. Preparation order

### Phase 1 - Teacher Talk A
Prepare first because it is the lowest-complexity structured educator offer and the foundation of the Teacher Talk series.

### Phase 2 - Teacher Talk B and C
Prepare only after A is delivery-ready.

### Phase 3 - ABC and Beyond™
Prepare authorised modules first where commercially useful, then the full programme.

### Phase 4 - Learning Language and Loving It™
Prepare after the shorter offers because of the greater programme, video-feedback and operational complexity.

Do not prepare all programmes simultaneously.

## 4. Readiness layers for every programme

### Layer A - Licence / permissions
Confirm from current Hanen resources:
- current licence/certification status,
- permitted audience,
- participant age/context,
- in-person / online permissions,
- participant limits,
- prerequisites,
- required materials/workbooks,
- required feedback/coaching elements,
- programme adaptation restrictions,
- recording restrictions,
- branding / promotional wording,
- participant documentation / certificate rules.

### Layer B - Content mastery
CJ should be able to explain:
- programme purpose,
- participant learning objectives,
- structure/session sequence,
- core concepts in plain language,
- why each activity exists,
- what each authorised video/example is intended to demonstrate,
- what participants should apply afterwards,
- common misunderstandings,
- boundaries / claims the programme does not support.

### Layer C - Facilitation mastery
CJ should be able to:
- explain without reading slides verbatim,
- facilitate activities at the intended pace,
- ask useful reflection questions,
- respond accurately to participant questions,
- redirect questions outside scope,
- keep the group on time,
- manage online/in-person logistics,
- preserve programme fidelity while sounding natural.

### Layer D - Operational readiness
Have ready:
- participant eligibility / registration process,
- attendance record,
- required workbooks/materials,
- venue/online setup,
- pre-programme instructions,
- privacy / video procedures where relevant,
- participant evaluation,
- completion documentation,
- payment / cancellation terms,
- post-training implementation follow-up.

### Layer E - Commercial readiness
Have ready:
- buyer-facing programme description,
- correct programme-owner wording,
- institutional quote,
- participant/material cost handling,
- cohort limits,
- travel rules,
- implementation follow-up option,
- answers to recognition / certificate / HRD questions.

## 5. Teacher Talk A readiness sequence

1. Obtain current licensed materials.
2. Confirm current programme version / update notices.
3. Read the full Leader Guide before editing any notes.
4. Map the session into blocks:
   - objective,
   - teaching point,
   - authorised media/activity,
   - participant practice/reflection,
   - action/application,
   - time allocation.
5. Create CJ's private trainer quick-reference notes without reproducing materials publicly.
6. Build common-question bank from the actual programme content.
7. Build scope / difficult-question bank.
8. Complete closed-book knowledge quiz.
9. Re-study weak areas.
10. Conduct timed sectional rehearsal.
11. Conduct full timed rehearsal.
12. Conduct hostile/difficult participant Q&A.
13. Check required participant materials / workbook logistics.
14. Test online and in-person setup as relevant.
15. Pass final readiness rubric >= 8.5/10 with no material fidelity error.
16. Only then approve a paid course date.

## 6. Readiness score principle

A high average score does not override a critical error.

Automatic NOT READY findings include:
- teaching a core concept inaccurately,
- omitting a mandatory programme element,
- using materials outside licence,
- making clinical/SLT claims outside APC scope,
- telling participants they become Hanen-certified providers when they do not,
- breaching recording/copyright restrictions,
- unable to answer or safely defer a common participant question,
- significant timing failure that prevents required content being delivered.

## 8. Evidence / claims layer

Maintain a separate evidence pack for buyer meetings:
- official Hanen programme description,
- relevant Hanen research summaries / peer-reviewed evidence,
- broader professional-development implementation evidence,
- Malaysia educator-need evidence.

Do not use the sales deck as a replacement for the licensed programme materials, and do not expose member-only training slides in sales demos.

## 9. Buyer fit before programme recommendation

Teacher Talk / LLLI / ABC should not be recommended merely because the buyer asks for “autism training”.

Route based on actual need:
- early-childhood interaction/language-support practice: Teacher Talk / LLLI may fit,
- emergent literacy/shared reading ages 3-5: ABC may fit,
- autism-specific inclusion/regulation/transitions/prompting/team consistency: APC's separate autism-specific educator support may fit better.
