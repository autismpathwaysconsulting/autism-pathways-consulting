## Copy-ready enquiry

**Subject:** Fixed-fee remote review of Malaysian parent-support service documents

Hello,

I operate Autism Pathways Consulting under CJ Special and Inclusive Consultancy, a Malaysian sole proprietorship. APC provides online parent education, pattern review, and structured home-support planning. It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical treatment, or crisis support.

I am seeking a fixed-fee remote legal review of a coordinated English and Bahasa Malaysia document pack before accepting another new paid client. The two proposed paid services are:

- RM350 for one 45-minute online parent session; and
- RM1,800 for four 60-minute online sessions, proposed to be billed as four RM450 milestones.

The intended commercial rule is no refunds for parent change of mind, cancellation, inactivity, or non-attendance, while preserving remedies that Malaysian law requires for APC non-delivery or qualifying service failure.

Please confirm whether your practising team can review:

1. Consumer Protection Act 1999 compliance, including service guarantees, unfair terms, cancellation, credits, refunds, limitation of liability, and electronic contracting.
2. Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transaction) Regulations 2024, including Bahasa Malaysia disclosures and the operating-address requirement.
3. Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and 2024 amendments, including parent and child information, sensitive-data consent, processors, cross-border transfers, retention, deletion, data breaches, and AI-assisted drafting.
4. The Parent Support Agreement, website Terms, Cancellation and Refund Policy, Privacy Notice, Cal.com wording, acceptance checkboxes, and durable-copy workflow.
5. Existing-client handling so new terms are not imposed retrospectively.
6. Online service to clients outside Malaysia and when country-specific advice is required.
7. Whether any professional licence, disclaimer, safeguarding, insurance, or other regulatory issue applies to this non-clinical parent-education service.

Please provide:

- the responsible lawyer's name and practising firm;
- confirmation that the lawyer currently holds a valid practising certificate;
- relevant consumer-contract and personal-data experience;
- the exact scope, exclusions, fee, tax, turnaround time, and number of revision rounds;
- whether the Bahasa Malaysia wording is included or needs a separate qualified reviewer; and
- the documents and business information you need from me.

The documents contain no real client case details. I can first supply the controlled draft pack and an implementation matrix. I will not send identifiable child or family information for this review.
