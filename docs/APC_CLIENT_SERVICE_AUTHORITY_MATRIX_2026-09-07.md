# APC Client Service Authority Matrix

## Internal release-control result

**Status:** Controlled draft. Not legal advice. Not authorisation to publish, deploy, accept payment, or process identifiable client data in a new system.
**Prepared:** 7 September 2026
**Authority version:** `APC-AUTHORITY-MATRIX-DRAFT-2026-09-07`

## Decision meanings

- **APPROVED:** The repository rule is clear enough for internal, non-client testing with fictional or fully de-identified data. It is not approval for public launch unless the row expressly says so.
- **HUMAN-ONLY:** CJ must review the facts and make or record the decision. Software may prepare a checklist or draft but may not decide or send it automatically.
- **BLOCKED:** Do not activate, publish, charge, or process identifiable data until the stated evidence and external review exist.

## Controlling rules

1. This matrix does not make a legal draft effective.
2. The most restrictive applicable result controls. An **APPROVED** template does not override a **BLOCKED** privacy, booking, payment, or international-service row.
3. No system may make a final decision about suitability, safeguarding, session use, programme closure, credit, refund, or a child's needs.
4. CJ's private residential address must not appear on the website, Cal.com, public terms, public invoices, public metadata, or other public material. APC needs a valid alternative operating address before online sale.
5. The no-change-of-mind-refund rule applies only prospectively after the parent receives and accepts the exact version before payment. Mandatory rights and remedies remain available where the law requires them.
6. Nothing here changes the agreement of an existing client without that client's express written agreement.

## Release sequence

1. Preserve containment: no public paid booking, direct checkout, identifiable D1 write, or identifiable AI prompt.
2. Reconcile current clients manually against their original agreements.
3. Complete provider, privacy, retention, security, deletion, incident, tax, and public-address evidence.
4. Obtain qualified Malaysian legal review and competent Bahasa Malaysia review of the coordinated documents.
5. Build the versioned onboarding and four-stage payment workflow using fictional data only.
6. Run and retain evidence from booking, acceptance, payment, export, deletion, recovery, and access-control tests.
7. Return to the Founder for explicit launch approval. Publication and deployment remain separate decisions.
