# APC Singapore Parent Pricing Strategy

Status: Internal working strategy. Use for upcoming Singapore enquiries unless a lower price has already been quoted/accepted.

## Recommended initial Singapore test pricing

### APC One-Concern Parent Session
**S$200**

Includes:
- 60-minute online consultation,
- short structured intake reviewed before the session,
- one working explanation/hypothesis,
- one agreed priority,
- 1–2 practical changes to test,
- APC One-Concern Action Note,
- one clarification message within 7 days, limited to the agreed concern/plan.

Position as a focused specialist consultation, not therapy or diagnosis.

### APC Home Implementation Programme
**S$980**

Includes:
- baseline intake and priority-setting,
- four 60-minute online sessions across approximately 6–8 weeks,
- personalised Home Support Plan,
- selected APC implementation tools,
- bounded WhatsApp clarification during the active programme,
- brief between-session implementation checks,
- plan review/adjustment across sessions,
- final maintenance summary,
- approximately 20–30 minute maintenance check about one month after Session 4.

Do not present as "4 x S$245 sessions". It is a programme fee for an implementation cycle.

## Why these prices are reasonable starting tests

Current Singapore comparators are not direct equivalents but provide market context:

- Nightingale Pediatrics lists 1-to-1 parent coaching at S$300 per 50 minutes.
- Morning Star lists its Incredible Years Autism Parenting Programme at S$988 per family before GST, including six 2-hour group sessions followed by individual coaching sessions.
- Beyond Words Singapore markets 60-minute virtual parent coaching and offers one-off consultations or 10-week coaching plans, although its current public services page does not display the price.

APC is intentionally not a regulated clinical service, so the initial test price should remain below premium clinical/private-provider pricing while reflecting specialist autism/inclusion expertise, preparation, written outputs and implementation support.

## Future Singapore pricing after proof

After several successful Singapore clients, strong referral behaviour and low price resistance, test:

- One-Concern Parent Session: **S$220–S$250**
- Home Implementation Programme: **S$1,100–S$1,200**

Do not move to this range solely because Singapore has higher general living costs. Use actual conversion, client feedback, demand and delivery load.

## Payment and tax wording

For an online Singapore consumer, quote and invoice in SGD if operationally convenient and receive payment through Wise/bank transfer as already planned.

Do not charge Singapore GST unless APC is actually registered/required to collect it. Under Singapore's Overseas Vendor Registration regime, overseas suppliers are generally required to register only if both global turnover exceeds S$1 million and annual B2C remote-service supplies to Singapore exceed S$100,000. Verify tax treatment if APC approaches those thresholds or the service model changes.

## What not to include in Singapore parent pricing

The parent packages do not include delivery of:
- Learning Language and Loving It™,
- Teacher Talk™,
- ABC and Beyond™,
- an official SCERTS programme.

Those remain separate educator/programme-owner offerings.
