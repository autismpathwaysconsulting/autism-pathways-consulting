# APC Educator PD — Participant Administration Pack

Status: Internal operational templates. Programme-specific completion/certificate wording must follow the relevant programme owner requirements.

## 1. Institutional registration intake

Collect only what is needed:
- organisation name,
- organisation contact,
- participant full name,
- participant email,
- role,
- age group/setting they work with,
- programme/training title,
- in-person/online,
- accessibility requirements,
- consent/acknowledgement of programme terms.

Do not collect student-identifying information during registration.

## 2. Pre-training participant message

Subject: [Programme] — participant information

Include:
- date,
- start/end time,
- venue/online link,
- arrival/login time,
- materials to bring,
- official workbook/material instructions,
- break/lunch information,
- recording rule,
- contact for logistics,
- reminder not to share identifiable child information unnecessarily during discussion.

For Hanen programmes, confirm any mandatory workbook/pre-reading requirements against current Leader Guide before sending.

## 3. Attendance register

Fields:
- organisation,
- programme,
- date,
- session number if applicable,
- participant name,
- arrival/time-in,
- departure/time-out,
- signature/attendance confirmation,
- completion status,
- notes on authorised make-up where applicable.

Do not mark completion automatically if programme attendance requirements are not met.

## 4. Training evaluation

Keep evaluation focused on usefulness and implementation rather than satisfaction alone.

### Immediate post-session items
Rate 1–5:
- The training objectives were clear.
- The examples/activities helped me understand the ideas.
- I can identify at least one change I intend to use in my setting.
- I feel clearer about when/how to use the approach.
- The training pace was manageable.

Open questions:
- What is one thing you plan to use?
- What still feels unclear?
- What may make implementation difficult in your setting?
- What follow-up would help?

### 3–4 week implementation follow-up
- Which agreed idea did you try?
- How often were you realistically able to use it?
- What made implementation easier?
- What got in the way?
- What changed in your own practice?
- What needs further clarification/coaching?

Do not convert this into a claim of guaranteed child outcomes.

## 5. Leadership implementation summary

For institutional buyers, provide a short aggregate summary where appropriate:
- number registered,
- number completing required attendance,
- main implementation intentions,
- common barriers reported,
- follow-up themes,
- recommended next organisational step.

Do not expose individual participant feedback without consent/clear prior agreement.

## 6. Participant completion record

Internal register fields:
- participant,
- programme,
- dates,
- hours attended,
- completion requirement met? yes/no,
- participant document issued? yes/no,
- document type,
- document ID,
- issue date,
- reissue history.

## 7. Certificate/document rule

Until Hanen provides written clarification/current Leader Guide confirms the exact participant-documentation process:
- do not promise a Hanen-branded certificate,
- do not state participants become Hanen-certified,
- do not create APC wording that implies provider licensure.

For APC-owned training, a truthful Certificate of Completion may include:
- participant name,
- APC-owned training title,
- date,
- duration/hours,
- completion criteria if relevant,
- APC business/trainer details,
- unique certificate/document ID.

It must not imply government, KPM, HRD Corp, MQA or regulated-profession accreditation unless actually held for that course.

## 8. Materials control

For third-party licensed programmes:
- each participant receives/uses required official materials as specified,
- do not email unrestricted copies of protected workbooks/slides,
- do not allow recording of restricted programme sessions,
- keep any licensed trainer-only material private.

## 9. Online delivery checklist

Before session:
- stable connection,
- presenter view tested,
- screen share tested,
- audio/video playback tested where authorised,
- breakout/activity setup tested if required,
- participant names visible,
- attendance process ready,
- recording disabled unless specifically allowed,
- backup contact channel available.

## 10. In-person delivery checklist

- room capacity suitable,
- screen/projector,
- speakers,
- HDMI/adapters,
- power/extension,
- seating/activity layout,
- participant materials/workbooks,
- attendance sheet,
- timing clock,
- water/break arrangements,
- privacy for any individual feedback component.

## 11. Incident / deviation log

Record if:
- mandatory content could not be completed,
- technology failure affected delivery,
- unauthorised recording occurred,
- participant missed required content,
- copyrighted material was accidentally distributed,
- safeguarding/privacy issue arose,
- programme had to deviate from the approved format.

Do not quietly mark the programme complete. Assess whether make-up/correction is required under the official programme rules.
