# Visual programme exploration

The revised page follows the existing APC schools page's split opening, warm cream and sage panels, dark teal typography and practical examples. It adds three original concept images, programme tabs, separate child and adult learning examples, and an interactive four-step coaching example.

Exploring a tab does not register interest. The explicit Add/Remove button changes the corresponding form checkbox. Both directions stay synchronised, and adding a second interest preserves an existing first preference. The page never assumes update consent. Submission, privacy and launch configuration are unchanged.

Programme tabs and coaching steps support arrow keys, Home, End and visible focus. All example panels remain readable without JavaScript. Motion is limited to selected-state and hover feedback, with reduced-motion support. Mobile variants include a compact image selector and a stacked layout below 360px.

## Image production

Built-in image generation was used for three still-life concept photographs. They contain no identifiable people, beneficiary imagery or claims of an existing programme. Source PNGs are preserved as generated assets; website copies are WebP in two responsive sizes.

- `programme-volunteering-480.webp` and `programme-volunteering-960.webp`: sealed rice and tins beside kraft and dark teal packing bags on a light oak table, warm cream backdrop and natural daylight.
- `programme-money-480.webp` and `programme-money-960.webp`: teal coin purse, generic coins, wooden play-shop groceries and a notebook on a warm wooden table.
- `programme-camp-480.webp` and `programme-camp-960.webp`: teal backpack, wicker beanbag basket, soft rope and wooden markers in a shaded garden with a cream day-play tent.

Prompt direction: photorealistic editorial still life, tactile wood and linen, coherent scale, soft natural light, cream/dark teal/sage palette, no people, logos, autism symbols or promotional text. These images illustrate possible activities. The page labels them as concepts rather than photographs of confirmed venues or sessions.
