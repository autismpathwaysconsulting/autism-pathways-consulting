# APC School & Educator Professional Development — B2B Terms Draft

Status: Internal commercial draft. Review before first paid institutional engagement. Not legal advice.

## 1. Parties and scope

The proposal/agreement should identify:
- Autism Pathways Consulting / CJ Special and Inclusive Consultancy,
- client organisation legal/business name,
- named programme or consultation scope,
- audience,
- format,
- dates/times,
- venue/platform,
- participant limit,
- implementation follow-up included/not included.

## 2. Programme fidelity

For third-party licensed programmes such as Hanen:
- APC will deliver only within CJ's current licence/certification and programme requirements,
- client may not require APC to remove mandatory elements, create unapproved modules or redistribute licensed materials,
- participant/material requirements must be met,
- programme-owner IP remains the programme owner's property.

## 3. APC-owned training

For APC-developed professional development:
- APC retains copyright in original slides, worksheets, frameworks and course materials unless otherwise agreed,
- participant copies are for personal/professional use within the agreed organisation,
- client may not resell, publish or reproduce APC training materials as its own course without written permission.

## 4. Fees

Proposal should itemise where relevant:
- professional fee,
- required participant materials/workbooks,
- venue/travel/accommodation,
- printing/courier,
- follow-up consultation,
- tax/other mandatory charges.

Do not describe the fee as HRD Corp claimable unless registration is actually in place.

## 5. Payment

Recommended starting commercial rule for review:
- booking is confirmed only after written acceptance and required deposit/payment,
- balance due by agreed date before delivery,
- materials with non-refundable third-party purchase/shipping costs may need to be paid in advance.

Exact deposit percentage and due dates require CJ approval before use.

## 6. Rescheduling/cancellation

Institutional training creates non-recoverable preparation/material/time costs. Create a separate B2B cancellation schedule rather than automatically applying parent-session terms.

Recommended structure for CJ/legal review:
- client substitution of participants allowed subject to programme eligibility/material requirements,
- reasonable reschedule notice required,
- third-party materials already ordered remain chargeable,
- late cancellation may trigger a percentage of professional fee,
- APC may reschedule for illness/emergency with alternative date/refund of undelivered professional fee.

Do not publish percentages until approved.

## 7. Participant attendance/completion

For each programme define:
- required attendance,
- completion activities,
- missed-session policy,
- whether make-up is possible under programme rules,
- what participant documentation is issued.

For Hanen programmes, follow Hanen requirements. Do not promise provider certification.

## 8. Professional-development records

APC may keep administrative records such as:
- participant name,
- organisation,
- programme,
- attendance,
- completion status,
- certificate/record ID,
- evaluation responses.

Only collect what is needed and manage it under APC privacy procedures.

## 9. Video feedback

If a programme involves classroom video:
- separate video/privacy schedule must be signed/acknowledged,
- organisation confirms required permissions are in place,
- footage is used only for the agreed professional-development purpose,
- no marketing/social-media reuse,
- transfer/storage/retention/deletion process agreed before recording.

## 10. Safeguarding

APC training does not replace the organisation's safeguarding obligations.

Client remains responsible for:
- student supervision,
- staff conduct,
- venue safety,
- emergency procedures,
- safeguarding reporting under its policies/law.

APC reserves the right to stop an activity if continuing would create a safeguarding or privacy concern.

## 11. Scope of service

APC provides education, consultation and professional development.

Unless separately and lawfully contracted by an appropriately registered professional, APC does not provide:
- medical diagnosis,
- psychological diagnosis/treatment,
- speech-language therapy/assessment,
- occupational therapy,
- crisis/emergency services.

## 12. Outcomes

APC does not guarantee:
- child developmental gains,
- behavioural outcomes,
- academic outcomes,
- staff performance outcomes,
- regulatory accreditation outcomes.

Where implementation evaluation is included, APC reports observed/self-reported implementation data within the agreed method and limitations.

## 13. Recording of training sessions

No audio/video recording of the training by client/participants unless APC and, where relevant, the programme owner explicitly permit it in writing.

This is particularly important for licensed third-party programme slides/videos/materials.

## 14. Confidentiality

Both parties should avoid unnecessary disclosure of identifiable child/family information during training.

Case discussions should be de-identified unless there is a lawful, necessary and agreed reason to share identifying information.

## 15. Recognition / accreditation wording

Proposal must state only recognition actually held.

Do not claim:
- KPM accreditation,
- HRD Corp claimability,
- MQA credit,
- regulated CPD points,
- international accreditation,
unless currently verified for that exact course.

## 16. Cross-border work

For delivery outside Malaysia, proposal is conditional on:
- programme-owner territorial permission where relevant,
- local professional/regulatory scope,
- privacy/data-transfer requirements,
- tax/payment/logistical requirements.

## 17. Client responsibilities

Client should provide:
- accurate participant numbers/roles,
- suitable venue/AV or online access,
- required participant materials,
- attendance coordination,
- consent/privacy compliance for any classroom footage,
- one organisation contact for logistics.

## 18. Force majeure / technology

Agreement should address material disruption outside reasonable control and how online-session platform failures will be handled.

## 19. Insurance

Before first paid institutional programme, CJ should review whether existing professional/commercial insurance covers:
- educator professional development,
- school consultation,
- third-party licensed programme delivery,
- classroom observation/video handling,
- online and international delivery if applicable.

## 20. Finalisation gate

Before this document becomes contract language:
- [ ] CJ approves deposit/payment rules.
- [ ] CJ approves cancellation schedule.
- [ ] Hanen participant-documentation/promotional clarification received.
- [ ] Video/privacy process finalised.
- [ ] Insurance reviewed.
- [ ] Malaysian legal/commercial review obtained if appropriate.
- [ ] Parent-service terms remain separate from B2B training terms.
