# Hanen Public Evidence & Positioning Pack

Status: Internal buyer-facing evidence preparation using public Hanen sources. Does not replace current licensed Leader Guides for delivery fidelity.

## 1. Teacher Talk™ Training Series

### Current public positioning
Hanen describes Teacher Talk™ as a flexible training series for early childhood educators working with children birth–5. It is available online and in person.

Current public Hanen capacity:
- online: up to 24 participants,
- in person: up to 30 participants.

Current public Hanen PD hours:
- full Teacher Talk series: 22.5 PD hours.

### Safe APC buyer wording

> "Teacher Talk™ is a Hanen professional-development series for early childhood educators. It focuses on strengthening everyday educator-child interactions that support early language, social and literacy development."

Do not call it autism-specific training.

## 2. Learning Language and Loving It™

### Current public positioning
Hanen describes LLLI as a programme for early childhood educators working with children birth–5, offered in person and online.

Current public programme components include:
- eight group sessions,
- individual video-feedback sessions,
- optional orientation session.

The public programme page currently states six personalised video-feedback sessions. Hanen's research summary also describes the programme as sustained professional learning with active participation, real-life application, coaching/feedback and collective participation from the same setting.

### Safe APC buyer wording

> "Learning Language and Loving It™ is a more intensive Hanen educator programme combining group professional learning with individual video feedback and implementation in the educator's own setting."

Do not promise an exact local delivery structure until the current CJ Leader Guide is checked because programme-owner resources may distinguish authorised variants or current leader options.

## 3. ABC and Beyond™

### Current public positioning
Hanen describes ABC and Beyond™ as an emergent-literacy programme for early childhood educators working with preschool and kindergarten children ages 3–5. Current public pages list online and in-person formats.

Hanen's current professional workshop page states that certified professionals gain a licence to deliver ABC and Beyond in flexible delivery models and use programme resources in individual consultations with educators.

### Safe APC buyer wording

> "ABC and Beyond™ is a Hanen educator programme focused on strengthening emergent-literacy practice, including shared reading and everyday language/literacy interactions with children ages 3–5."

Do not list authorised module structures from memory. Verify them against CJ's current agreement/Leader Guide before quoting a module.

## 4. LLLI research framing

Hanen's current research summary describes the programme as professional learning that includes:
- sustained study over time,
- active educator participation,
- simulated and real-life practice,
- mentoring/coaching,
- immediate feedback,
- collective participation by educators from the same setting.

Hanen lists empirical studies examining educator interaction practices, peer support, emergent literacy and implementation.

### Safe APC claim

> "LLLI has a published research base examining changes in educator practice and aspects of children's interaction/language/literacy engagement. APC does not promise that any individual child will achieve a particular developmental outcome."

Avoid turning the research into a sales claim that the programme guarantees child language gains.

## 5. Programme differentiation for buyers

### Teacher Talk™
Best buyer fit to explore when:
- early-childhood team wants a shorter/flexible professional-development series,
- problem concerns everyday educator-child interaction/language/social/literacy support,
- buyer is not asking for autism-specific training.

### LLLI™
Best buyer fit to explore when:
- organisation wants deeper sustained practice change,
- leadership can support multiple group sessions plus individual feedback,
- buyer accepts the programme's greater time/implementation commitment.

### ABC and Beyond™
Best buyer fit to explore when:
- children are roughly ages 3–5,
- organisation wants emergent-literacy/shared-reading practice strengthened,
- literacy is the central need rather than generic autism support.

### APC autism-specific educator support
Best buyer fit when:
- concern is autism-specific participation, regulation, transitions, prompting/independence or team consistency,
- a Hanen language/literacy programme would not directly solve the buyer's stated problem.

## 6. Claims APC should avoid

Do not say:
- Teacher Talk is an autism programme,
- LLLI is speech therapy,
- ABC and Beyond is a reading-intervention certification for children,
- participants become Hanen-certified providers,
- Hanen guarantees child developmental gains,
- CJ may alter licensed content freely,
- all programmes can be delivered to any age group.

## 7. Sales-deck evidence line

Recommended concise wording:

> "Where a Hanen programme fits the buyer's need, APC uses the official licensed programme rather than recreating a generic workshop. Where the need is autism-specific rather than early-language/literacy professional development, APC keeps that work in a separate autism-specific consultation/PD lane."
