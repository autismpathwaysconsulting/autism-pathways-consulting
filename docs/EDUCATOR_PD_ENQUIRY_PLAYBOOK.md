# APC School & Educator PD Enquiry Playbook

Status: Weekend enquiry launch
Purpose: respond consistently, protect scope, learn what buyers actually need, and avoid selling a programme before fit is clear.

## 1. First response objective

The first reply should do three things only:

1. acknowledge the organisation and problem,
2. clarify the setting and desired change,
3. route to the right next step.

Do not immediately quote a Hanen programme simply because the enquirer asks for “autism training”.

## 2. Safe first-reply structure

Thank them for reaching out.

Clarify:
- organisation type,
- age group,
- approximate number of staff,
- who would attend,
- one or two recurring problems they want staff to handle better,
- whether they want a one-off workshop, structured programme, consultation, or are unsure,
- preferred in-person / online format,
- preferred timeline,
- whether the organisation requires HRD Corp claimability or another formal recognition.

Then offer a short leadership / needs conversation if the request is potentially suitable.

## 3. Core qualifying questions

### Organisation
- What type of setting is this?
- Where is it located?
- Which age group(s) do you support?
- Approximately how many educators would attend?

### Problem
- What is happening now that you would like the team to handle better?
- Is this mainly about early language/interaction, emergent literacy, autism-specific implementation, team consistency, or something else?
- Is the issue organisation-wide or centred on one class/team?

### Desired outcome
- What would be different after useful training?
- What would leadership want staff to do more consistently?
- Is follow-up implementation support important?

### Procurement
- Who makes the training decision?
- Is there a training budget?
- Is HRD Corp claimability required, preferred, or irrelevant?
- Does the organisation require internal CPD/PD documentation?

### Format
- In person or online?
- Weekday / weekend / school holiday?
- Target date?

## 4. Routing logic

### Teacher Talk™ may fit when
- buyer is an early childhood setting,
- need concerns educator-child interaction, language opportunities or related early-years practice,
- buyer wants structured professional development,
- participant prerequisites / materials / format can be met.

Do not present it as autism-specific.

### Learning Language and Loving It™ may fit when
- buyer is an early childhood setting,
- leadership wants deeper practice change rather than one workshop,
- the organisation can support the full programme structure and individual video-feedback requirements,
- staff can commit to the programme schedule.

### ABC and Beyond™ may fit when
- buyer supports children roughly 3–5 years old,
- need concerns emergent literacy, shared reading, vocabulary, story understanding, print knowledge or phonological awareness,
- buyer can meet the requirements of the chosen full programme / authorised module.

Do not improvise modules outside Hanen-authorised formats.

### APC autism-specific consultation / PD may fit when
- concern is inclusion, regulation, transitions, participation, prompting/independence, team consistency or parent-school collaboration,
- buyer needs autism-specific implementation support rather than Hanen early-childhood language/literacy curriculum.

This must remain APC-owned material. Do not copy Hanen, SCERTS or other proprietary training resources into the course.

### Refer / decline when
- request is diagnosis,
- request is speech-language assessment or therapy,
- request is psychology / medical / crisis work,
- buyer asks CJ to certify them as a Hanen/SCERTS provider,
- safeguarding/privacy requirements cannot be met,
- requested adaptation would breach programme fidelity/licensing,
- target population or geography falls outside confirmed permission.

## 5. Safe buyer FAQ answers

### “Is this accredited?”
Use:

“APC provides professional development and consultation. The Hanen programmes listed are official programmes I am certified/licensed to provide. APC is not currently advertising the training as KPM-accredited, HRD Corp claimable, or as an accredited Malaysian qualification.”

### “Will staff be certified?”
Use:

“Participants are completing professional development. They do not become Hanen-certified providers or trainers by attending a programme I deliver. Participant documentation will follow the requirements of the specific programme.”

Until Hanen confirms the exact certificate process, do not promise a Hanen-branded certificate.

### “Can staff use what they learn?”
Use:

“Yes. The purpose of the educator programmes is practical application in the educational setting. Staff are not being licensed to train others in the programme.”

### “Is this autism training?”
Use:

“The Hanen early-childhood programmes are not autism-specific. APC's autism-specific consultation and educator professional development are separate services.”

### “Do you provide speech therapy?”
Use:

“No. APC is an education, consultation and professional-development service. I do not provide speech-language therapy through APC.”

### “Is it HRD Corp claimable?”
Use:

“Not currently. I am assessing whether HRD Corp registration would be useful based on what organisations actually require.”

Record every time this question is asked.

### “What outcomes do you guarantee?”
Use:

“APC does not guarantee child outcomes. For professional development, the immediate focus is educator learning, implementation and what changes in practice. Where appropriate, we can agree how implementation will be reviewed.”

### “Can you train our team in Singapore / overseas?”
Use:

“I can review the request, but cross-border delivery depends on the specific programme licence, format and local requirements. I will confirm this before accepting the engagement.”

## 6. What to log from every enquiry

Create one row per organisation:

- date,
- organisation,
- location/country,
- buyer role,
- organisation type,
- learner age group,
- team size,
- main problem,
- desired outcome,
- requested programme if any,
- recommended route,
- in-person/online,
- timeline,
- HRD required? yes/no/preferred,
- formal CPD recognition required? what type,
- budget disclosed?,
- key objection,
- next step,
- won/lost/pending,
- reason lost.

## 7. Demand-validation thresholds

Do not infer demand from likes, Story votes or compliments.

Stronger signals:
1. decision-maker takes a meeting,
2. organisation shares a real staff problem,
3. buyer asks for programme structure / quote,
4. buyer shares timing / cohort size,
5. buyer requests procurement documents,
6. buyer agrees to a paid pilot or deposit.

## 8. Weekend response rule

For the first weekend, the aim is not to close every lead immediately.

Aim to learn:
- which problem language triggers enquiries,
- which buyer roles respond,
- whether early-years Hanen programmes or autism-specific APC support attracts stronger demand,
- whether HRD claimability is actually required,
- whether buyers prefer institutional cohorts or individual seats.

## 9. Claims that require escalation before answering

Pause and verify before answering questions about:
- official participant certificate wording,
- Hanen logo use,
- programme adaptation,
- translations,
- cross-border Hanen delivery,
- HRD registration of Hanen material,
- KPM recognition,
- MQA credit,
- professional/regulatory CPD points,
- use of SCERTS branding/materials.

Do not improvise an answer in a sales conversation.
