# APC School & Educator PD — Internal Pricing Draft

Status: **Working pricing hypothesis only. Not market-validated. Do not publish as a fixed price list yet.**

Purpose: ensure CJ can respond coherently if a serious institutional enquiry asks for budget guidance before the first pilot.

## Pricing principles

1. Price the organisation outcome and delivery workload, not only CJ's live speaking hours.
2. Third-party participant materials are separate and billed transparently.
3. Full LLLI / ABC programmes require substantial individual feedback workload and must be scoped by cohort size.
4. Do not use fake “normal price / special price” anchors.
5. Do not promise HRD Corp claimability.
6. Keep first-pilot pricing reviewable after real buyer feedback.

## Working institutional quote bands

### 1. Teacher Talk™ Training A
Official duration: 7.5 hours.

**Working starting quote:** RM4,800–RM6,000 for an institutional cohort up to approximately 12 educators, plus required participant materials and travel/venue costs where applicable.

Rationale:
- 7.5 live hours,
- programme preparation/refresher time,
- buyer needs call,
- logistics/admin,
- participant communication,
- attendance/PD documentation,
- post-training evaluation,
- optional implementation follow-up priced separately unless included.

For cohorts materially above 12, quote after confirming workload/format rather than simply absorbing all additional participants at the same fee.

**Do not publish this range until CJ approves it.**

### 2. Teacher Talk™ B or C
Use a similar starting structure to Training A because each is also 7.5 hours, adjusted for preparation, participant prerequisites and materials.

### 3. Full Teacher Talk™ A+B+C
Official full series: 22.5 PD hours.

**Working planning band:** roughly RM13,500–RM17,000 + required participant materials, before any bespoke implementation add-on.

Do not automatically discount the full series. If a package saving is offered later, it should reflect genuine reductions in repeated sales/admin effort.

### 4. APC focused autism-specific educator PD
For APC-owned live training, until the curriculum is fully productised:

**2-hour focused session:** internal planning band RM1,800–RM2,800 per organisation.

**Half-day / 3–4 hour session:** internal planning band RM2,800–RM4,500 per organisation.

Possible implementation follow-up:
- 60–90 minute team/leadership review: RM700–RM1,200 depending on preparation/reporting.

These are market-test hypotheses, not validated prices.

### 5. Learning Language and Loving It™
Do **not** use a simple day rate.

Full programme workload depends on:
- 15–20 group-training hours under the licensed format,
- cohort size,
- mandatory individual video-feedback sessions,
- scheduling/coordination,
- video/consent administration,
- travel or online delivery logistics.

Use a bespoke institutional proposal after confirming exact participant count and programme format.

Internal expectation: likely a **five-figure institutional engagement** once the true feedback workload is included. Do not quote a final band until CJ has refreshed the programme and modelled the per-participant feedback hours from the current Leader Guide.

### 6. ABC and Beyond™ full programme
Same principle as LLLI.

Full programme includes substantial group hours plus individual video feedback. Quote only after:
- cohort size,
- full programme vs authorised module,
- delivery format,
- number/length of required feedback sessions,
- participant materials,
- video/privacy workflow
are confirmed.

### 7. ABC authorised modules
Potentially useful as a lower-risk institutional entry offer.

Price each module based on:
- number of group sessions,
- required video feedback,
- number of participants,
- preparation/admin burden.

Do not price “per 3-hour class” while ignoring mandatory feedback workload.

## Materials policy

Recommended wording in quotes:

“Required third-party participant materials are charged separately at the current purchase, shipping and applicable transaction cost. APC will confirm the material cost before ordering.”

Do not add an undisclosed margin to licensed Hanen materials unless legally/commercially appropriate and clearly reflected in the quotation.

## Travel / venue

Before first quote, CJ should set:
- included travel radius,
- mileage / transport rule outside that radius,
- parking/tolls,
- accommodation rule for interstate engagements,
- whether venue/AV is client-provided.

## Deposit/payment decision still needed

Before the first paid engagement, CJ must approve:
- deposit percentage,
- balance due date,
- cancellation/reschedule schedule,
- treatment of non-refundable materials already ordered.

## How to answer “How much?” this weekend

If fit is not yet clear:

“Professional-development fees depend on the programme, cohort size, format, participant materials and whether implementation follow-up is included. I first confirm the need and participant group, then send a fixed written scope and quote so there are no surprise additions.”

If the buyer needs an indicative budget for Teacher Talk A:

“For planning purposes, I am currently modelling a 7.5-hour institutional Teacher Talk A engagement in roughly the RM4,800–RM6,000 professional-fee range for a smaller whole-team cohort, before required participant materials and any travel. I would confirm a fixed quote after the needs discussion.”

Use this only as an **indicative planning range**, not a published tariff.

## Market-validation questions

For every serious buyer, record:
- Was the range lower / expected / higher than expected?
- What did they compare it with?
- Would they prefer per-person or organisation pricing?
- Is HRD Corp claimability important enough to change the purchase decision?
- Would follow-up implementation support increase value or feel like an unnecessary add-on?
- What budget authority does the decision-maker have?

After 5–10 qualified buyer conversations, revise this file using real evidence.
