# APC Blog Content Architecture

Status: working content plan for website expansion.

## Purpose

Build a useful APC article library without mixing unrelated audiences. Keep articles practical, evidence-aware and easy to scan.

## Category 1 — Practical support at home

For parents looking for help with repeated daily situations.

Initial topics:
- When transitions keep going wrong even with warnings
- Prompting versus independence: how to notice when adults are doing too much
- When screen-time rules keep breaking down
- Morning routines: what to look at before adding more reminders
- Why a strategy can work one week and fail the next
- What to do when a plan is not helping

Primary CTA: relevant free resource / First Step Call when fit is unclear.

## Category 2 — Malaysia autism navigation and local guides

For families trying to navigate real-world options in Malaysia.

Initial topics:
- What “autism-friendly” should actually mean for families
- Autism-friendly places in KL / PJ: what support is really available
- What to ask a school before deciding whether it is a good fit
- Who does what? Understanding educator, therapist and medical roles
- What to prepare before your first parent consultation

Primary CTA: relevant resource, not automatic paid conversion.

## Category 3 — School and educator practice

For teachers, aides, SEN teams and school leaders.

Initial topics:
- Finished work does not always mean independent work
- How over-prompting can hide what a learner can do
- Supporting transitions without relying only on verbal reminders
- Regulation support versus expecting a child to “calm down”
- Parent-school consistency: what actually needs to match
- What implementation follow-up adds after professional development

Primary CTA: School & Educator Support / enquiry route.

## Category 4 — Understanding APC's approach

For readers deciding whether APC fits.

Initial topics:
- Why APC focuses on implementation, not just advice
- What happens when a parent plan does not work
- What a One-Concern Parent Session is designed to do
- What happens across the Home Implementation Programme
- When APC may recommend another professional instead
- What evidence-informed means in APC's work

Primary CTA: Services / First Step Call.

## Tone

Use A+B across parent and educator articles:
- practical first
- warm second
- short paragraphs
- plain language
- specific examples
- no clinical overclaiming
- no long motivational introductions

## Article structure

1. Clear problem-led title
2. 2–3 sentence opening showing why the problem is hard
3. What may be happening
4. What to notice before changing the plan
5. 2–4 practical considerations
6. When to adjust / stop / seek another route
7. Short scope-safe CTA
8. Sources where factual / research claims are used

## First publishing batch

Recommended first four articles, one from each category:
1. Parent: **What to Do When an Autism Strategy Is Not Helping**
2. Malaysia guide: **Autism-Friendly? What Families Should Check First**
3. Educator: **Finished Work Does Not Always Mean Independent Work**
4. APC approach: **Why Good Advice Can Still Fail at Home**

These four reuse themes already developed in APC content, reducing research and production burden while creating a coherent blog foundation.

## Content reuse rule

A strong article can later become:
- Reel script
- carousel
- parent resource link
- school-enquiry follow-up
- newsletter / social caption

Do not publish the exact same wording everywhere. Reuse the idea and evidence, then adapt format and length.
