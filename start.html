Discovery Call

What happens during a 15-minute Discovery Call

The Discovery Call is a short clarity and fit check. CJ listens to your main concern, checks whether APC support is the right next step, and recommends what makes sense next.

1

We understand the concern

CJ briefly listens to what is happening, what feels difficult, and what you are hoping to understand.

2

We look for one possible pattern

You receive one useful reframe and one initial direction, not a full consultation or mini-programme.

3

We recommend the next step

CJ may recommend a Parent Strategy Session, a parent support option, observation first, or another suitable direction.

APC is parent education and guidance. The Discovery Call is not therapy, diagnosis, crisis support, or a personalised behaviour plan.
