Free parent support tool

APC Calm Companion

A free, mobile-friendly parent tool for routines, communication, emotional regulation, transitions, and difficult moments. No download from the App Store is required.

Open Free Tool Install on phone
What it helps with

Quick support when you are not sure what to say or do next.

The tool is designed for parents who need simple, practical prompts during daily challenges. It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, or crisis support.

✓ Visual routines
✓ Calm-down tools
✓ Parent scripts
✓ Communication support
✓ iPhone & Android friendly
✓ No download required

Use it like an app

Open the tool from your phone home screen once installed.

  • 🫁 Calm breathing support
  • ⏳ Visual timers and routines
  • 💬 Communication prompts
  • ❤️ Parent reassurance
Install on phone

Save the free tool to your phone in about 1 minute.

Parents can add the APC Calm Companion to the phone home screen so it opens like a normal app. This is useful during stressful moments when you need quick access.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open the tool in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
  5. Open it from your home screen anytime.

Android

  1. Open the tool in Chrome.
  2. Tap the 3-dot menu.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Install or Add.
  5. Open it from your home screen anytime.
