When to use it

Quick support when you are not sure what to say or do next.

During transitions

Use visual steps and simple language when moving between activities.

During overwhelm

Find calmer scripts and options when your child is struggling to regulate.

During routines

Support daily tasks like getting ready, hygiene, bedtime, or leaving the house.

Use it like an app

Save APC Calm to your phone in about 1 minute.

Parents can add APC Calm Companion to the phone home screen, so it opens quickly during stressful moments.

iPhone / iPad

Open in Safari

Tap the share button, choose “Add to Home Screen,” then tap Add.

Android

Open in Chrome

Tap the three-dot menu, choose “Add to Home screen,” then confirm.
