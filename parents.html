What we work through together

  1. Describe the moment. What happened before, during and after? Include your child's perspective wherever possible.
  2. Consider what may be contributing. Separate what you observed from possible explanations.
  3. Choose a workable change. Make the next step specific enough to try in your family's routine.
  4. Review and adjust. In ongoing support, use what happened to decide whether the plan needs changing.

APC provides parent education and guidance. It does not provide diagnosis, therapy, medical care or guaranteed outcomes, and works alongside your child's existing professionals.
