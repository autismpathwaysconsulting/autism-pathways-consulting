The APC HOME steps

Each coaching session usually follows these four steps.

  1. H

    Hold back and notice

    Slow down one hard moment and look at it together, without blame.

  2. O

    Open up the why

    Look at communication, senses, routines and what was being asked.

  3. M

    Make one change

    Choose one small change to how you respond or how things are set up.

  4. E

    Ease it in together

    Try it with everyone at home, then adjust it as you go.

Questions parents ask

What is included?

Four one-to-one online sessions over 6 weeks, in weeks 1, 2, 4 and 6. One session can be just for your partner, helper or grandparent, in English or Bahasa Malaysia. A visual support and script for one hard moment, with the first version within 2 working days of session 1. Notes after every session. WhatsApp replies the same working day, for the 6 weeks and a settling month after. Your written plan within 48 hours of the last session. With your consent, a note to your child’s therapists and school. If the plan is not usable at home by session 4, CJ adds up to 2 more sessions at no cost.

Is this therapy?

No. APC is parent education and coaching, not therapy, diagnosis or treatment. It works with your child’s therapists and school, not instead of them.

Do we need a diagnosis?

No. You can book the free call, use the Quick Check or join Parent Coaching whether or not your child has a diagnosis, or while you are waiting for one.

Will it work alongside my child’s therapist?

That is the aim. With your written consent, CJ sends a short note to your child’s therapists and school at the start and a summary at the end, and offers them a call, so the plan at home may fit what they already do.

What if my child does not use speech?

That is fine. The plan looks at every way your child communicates, such as gestures, pictures, sounds, devices or behaviour, and visual supports are made to match.

How are fees shared?

Privately, after the free call, once CJ checks it is a good fit. Nothing is paid before your first session. If session 1 is not right, tell CJ within 7 days and you pay nothing. After that, you only pay for the parts you have started. CJ confirms fit and availability first, then sends payment details privately. Malaysia: bank transfer or DuitNow QR. Other countries: from your own Wise account or by international bank transfer.
