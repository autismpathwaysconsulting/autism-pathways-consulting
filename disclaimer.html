Educational and supportive consultation services.

Overview

Autism Pathways Consulting provides educational and supportive consultation services for parents, caregivers, schools, and educators.

Not Medical or Diagnostic Services

  • APC services are not medical services
  • APC services are not psychological services
  • APC services are not therapy services
  • APC does not provide formal diagnosis
  • APC does not replace professional medical or mental health care

Educational Nature of Recommendations

Recommendations shared are educational and informational in nature and are based on the information voluntarily provided during sessions.

No Emergency or Crisis Support

APC does not provide emergency, crisis, or urgent mental health services. If immediate medical, psychological, or safety support is required, please contact appropriate emergency or healthcare services.

Individual Differences

Every child and family situation is unique. Strategies and recommendations may not produce identical outcomes across all situations. APC does not guarantee specific behavioural, developmental, educational, or emotional outcomes.
